682 - a publisher nobody had measured, a reader nobody had tested: sigma26 and pdf2mmd
=====================================================================================

20 documents of SIGMA (Symmetry, Integrability and Geometry), 597 pages,
drilled by pdfdrill to the probe stage only -- PDF, LaTeX source, fonts,
facts, and no geometry at all. inkdrill needs a reader's opinion to
cross-check, so this round produced one: pdfdrill's pdf2mmd, run here
over all 20 (3.5 min), and the pages rendered at 400 dpi (597 pages,
38 s).

====================================================================
1. THE READER'S GEOMETRY AGAINST THE INK
====================================================================

Every line region, page-level `diagram` and `fill` mapped onto the 400
dpi render through `page_frames` (MathPix px are the CropBox;
inspect/pages is the MediaBox), then ink counted inside and outside.

    pdf2mmd, 20 documents, 597 pages    99.99-100.00% of ink inside a region
    MathPix, sigma26-075, 26 pages      98.46%

    0 of 597 pages refused by `page_frames`: pdf2mmd uses MathPix's
    250 dpi page, so the 1.6 ratio to a 400 dpi render needs no
    special case.

THE MATHPIX RESIDUAL IS ONE OBJECT, REPEATED. Exactly 10,590 px per
page, every page:

    y 263-265, 2,521 px wide    the rule under the running head
    y 3980-3982, 1,009 px wide  the footnote separator

MathPix emits no line for either; pdf2mmd records both, as `fills` read
from the PDF's vector operators. A publisher's own furniture, invisible
to the reader that only sees a raster, found by ink alone.

A MISTAKE OF MINE, CORRECTED BEFORE REPORTING. The first pass counted
only `lines[].region` and called sigma26-082 p20 "100% uncovered". That
page is a full-page TikZ figure which pdf2mmd records as one `diagram`
plus 511 `fills` -- page-level keys the measure ignored. Six documents'
apparent residuals were mine, not the reader's. **A reader's geometry is
not only its lines.**

====================================================================
2. THE VOCABULARY DEFECT, MEASURED AND FIXED
====================================================================

pdf2mmd's `math` meant the OPPOSITE of MathPix's. In pdf2mmd it was
prose carrying inline maths ("Definition 1.1. A graphon $\Gamma$ = ...");
in MathPix `math` is display maths and prose stays `text`. Both
`inlinectx.load_spans` and inkdrill's copy of the host rule SKIP `math`
as display maths.

    inline spans over the 20 documents          36,813
    on lines typed `math`, hence invisible      29,172   = 79.2%
    the control: sigma26-075 (MathPix)          0 of 565 lost

It ran both ways: `equation` lines (pdf2mmd's display maths) carried
6,402 spans that were being mined as if they were prose.

Reported to pdfdrill with those numbers; shipped the same day as their
844/844b (`docmodel_six.to_lines_json`, translated at the EXPORT
boundary so the reader keeps its own words):

    math -> text · equation -> math · caption -> figure_label
    formula -> text (166 lines, their finding, not mine)
    rotated_text KEPT (an arXiv stamp is not a sentence; their
    paragraph.py excludes it by that name)

RE-MEASURED HERE AFTER THE CHANGE, same 20 documents, same method:

    inline spans invisible to load_spans   79.2%  ->  17.7%
    lines with no `text_display` key       many   ->  0 of 24,943
    types outside MathPix's 31             2      ->  0
    ink coverage                           unchanged (99.99-100%)

The remaining 17.7% (6,580 spans) sit on lines now typed `math` -- they
ARE display maths, and skipping them is the rule working, not loss.

Their own A/B against an author's LaTeX as gold (arXiv 1102.1889):
Equations 961 -> 89 against a gold of 83, the worst offenders being the
paper's ABSTRACT filed as display maths.

====================================================================
3. WHAT THIS LEAVES
====================================================================

  * sigma26-075 keeps BOTH readings: `sigma26-075.lines.json` is the
    MathPix one (restored -- my regeneration had overwritten it), and
    `sigma26-075.lines.pdf2mmd.json` beside it. The only document in the
    library where the two readers can be compared row for row.
  * The other 19 carry pdf2mmd readings and 400 dpi renders.
  * pdfdrill reports 25 documents still holding a pre-844 reading;
    the discriminator is a content line with no `text_display` key.

====================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
====================================================================

  * Coverage is INK INSIDE A RECTANGLE. It says nothing about whether
    the reader's text is right, only that it claimed the right places.
    99.99% coverage with a wrong reading is possible and not excluded.
  * The 17.7% residual spans were not individually checked; they are
    display-maths lines by type, not by inspection.
  * One publisher, 20 documents, one reader version. The header-rule
    finding is SIGMA's layout, not a general claim about MathPix.
  * No marks were produced for these documents: the formula pipeline
    has not been run on a pdf2mmd reading yet.
