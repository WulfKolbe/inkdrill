641 - null test: does the MathPix-size resample move the five-tuple?
=====================================================================

NULL TEST, NO FIX. Nothing here was tuned, and nothing found was
repaired.

  QUESTION. A published table / image / host-line residual compares a
  locally rendered crop against a MathPix one. Those differ in two
  ways at once: the content may genuinely disagree, and one side has
  been through a resample. Take a crop we rendered OURSELVES, apply
  ONLY the resample, and see whether the five-tuple moves. Systematic
  movement means the resample is a constant confound inside every one
  of those residuals. Scattered movement means it is not.

THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION ASKED

THE COMPONENT DELTA IS SCATTERED, NOT SYSTEMATIC. 18 of 20 crops are
unchanged, the mean is -0.100 against a spread of 0.300, and the
median is 0. The resample is NOT sitting inside every published
table / image / host-line residual as a constant offset. It is inside
about one in ten of them.

TWO QUALIFICATIONS, BOTH AGAINST MY OWN CONCLUSION.

  1. THE DIRECTION IS NOT SCATTERED, ONLY THE INCIDENCE. Both non-zero
     deltas are -1 and neither is +1. Components are lost, never
     gained, which is what a downsample should do physically -- two
     glyphs whose gap falls below a pixel merge, and nothing splits.
     So the right description is not "random noise" but "a rare,
     one-directional loss". At n=20 with 2 events, 2-0 cannot be
     separated from chance; the physical argument is what makes the
     asymmetry believable, not the count.

  2. COMPONENTS ARE THE CLEANEST CHANNEL OF THE FIVE, AND THE
     QUESTION ONLY ASKED ABOUT THEM. Holes move on 4 of 20 crops, in
     BOTH directions, and by up to 4: FO0537 goes 12 -> 8, while
     FO1638 goes 0 -> 2 and FO2580 3 -> 5. That is a larger and
     messier effect than the component channel shows, and any residual
     that reads the hole count carries it. It is consistent with what
     out/591 and out/496 recorded about the hole channel near a
     speckle floor -- at 250 dpi a counter is a few pixels, and
     LANCZOS ringing plus q92 can both close one and open one.

  `stacked` and `centred` each moved on the same single crop (FO2784),
  and `offset` did not move at all.
