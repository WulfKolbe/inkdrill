652 - the per-length floor: it fixes the confound and finds no signal
======================================================================

out/651 closed by naming the single score floor as the bug. Tested.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CALIBRATION POPULATION IS WHAT MAKES THIS POSSIBLE

The floor is fitted on rows whose LaTeX is CHARACTER-IDENTICAL to the
author's arXiv source. For those the only remaining difference between
our render and the page is the RENDERING, so their score-versus-length
curve is the divergence with nothing else in it. 572 such rows.

That population could not have been built from MathPix's output alone,
and it is the first thing in this chain that needed the e-print.

    length   MATCHED  median   p10      KNOWN-CLASS  median   p10
      0-14     170     0.977  0.935          35       0.982  0.949
     15-24     137     0.972  0.863         180       0.973  0.894
     25-34     108     0.965  0.854         204       0.959  0.836
     35-49      90     0.937  0.777         299       0.946  0.824
     50-69      38     0.886  0.805         335       0.906  0.797
     70-99      22     0.914  0.746         213       0.861  0.754

THE TWO COLUMNS ARE THE SAME CURVE. Rows MathPix rewrote score like
rows it copied, at every length. out/651's length-confound conclusion
is confirmed on a second cut of the data.

    floor(L) = 1.0806 - 0.1685*log10(L)

        L=10   0.912        L=80    0.760
        L=20   0.861        L=160   0.709   EXTRAPOLATED
        L=40   0.811        L=274   0.670   EXTRAPOLATED

    the single floor: 0.7896

--------------------------------------------------------------------
IT FIXES THE CONFOUND, AND THE FIX IS LARGE

    POOR_FIT by length, single floor    {0:7, 25:40, 50:37, 75:44,
                                         100:35, 125:23, 150:36}
    POOR_FIT by length, per-length      {0:52, 25:70, 50:35, 75:21,
                                         100:16, 125:12, 150:7}

The single floor RISES with length and the per-length one FALLS. 222
rows become 213, but 84 are dropped and 75 are new -- two-thirds of
the set turned over.

THE SINGLE FLOOR WAS WRONG IN BOTH DIRECTIONS, as predicted. At three
characters it demanded 0.79 where correct rows deliver 0.96, so it saw
7 flags in the whole 0-24 band. At 150 characters it demanded 0.79
where correct rows deliver ~0.71, so it flagged 36 rows for being long.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
AND IT FINDS NO SOURCE SIGNAL. THE INSTRUMENT DOES NOT SEE THE LATEX

                        single 0.7896     per-length
    matched (n=572)          5.8%            10.3%
    known   (n=1467)        12.4%             9.2%

The 2.1x apparent enrichment collapses, and reverses: under the
per-length floor a row whose LaTeX is character-identical to the
author's is flagged SLIGHTLY MORE OFTEN than one MathPix rewrote.
Length-controlled it holds at every band -- 8.8/6.0, 11.6/8.2,
10.0/8.9.

SO THE FLAG IS NOT ABOUT THE SOURCE AT ANY FLOOR. It measures how well
our render of an expression sits on the ink, and that is dominated by
rendering divergence whatever the LaTeX says. A better floor does not
turn a fit measurement into an OCR measurement; it only stops the fit
measurement from being a length measurement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS WORTH ANYWAY

A uniform ~10% flag rate across every length instead of 1% to 42%. The
residual report stops being a list of long expressions. And the 0-24
band goes from 7 flagged rows to 52, which is where a misfit CANNOT be
blamed on accumulation -- a three-character expression has nothing to
accumulate.

The new short flags, score against their own floor:

    FO0255  L=3  0.935 / 0.960   t=0
    FO1180  L=3  0.942 / 0.960   a=0
    FO0651  L=5  0.871 / 0.960   |a|=1
    FO2850  L=5  0.950 / 0.960   J B=B J
    FO2297  L=6  0.886 / 0.949   a(X)=0
    FO2854  L=6  0.870 / 0.949   J B=-B J

ONE CHECKED AGAINST THE SOURCE AND IT DOES NOT EXPLAIN THE SCORE. The
author writes `JB = BJ` twice; MathPix writes `J B=B J`. In math mode
TeX IGNORES the space, so the two render identically and the
substitution cannot be why FO2850 scores 0.950 or FO2854 0.870. These
rows need an eye, exactly as the eighteen did.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT CLAIMED, AND ONE LIMIT THAT BITES

  * ABOVE 99 CHARACTERS THE FLOOR IS EXTRAPOLATED. The longest
    author-matched row is 99 characters and 201 known-class rows are
    longer than that. The curve is fitted on six buckets and used on
    a range half again as wide, which is exactly the kind of reach
    this project keeps recording as a population error. The 150+
    figures should be read as the weakest in the table.
  * A LOG-LINEAR FIT ON SIX POINTS. The p10 series is not monotone
    (0.777 then 0.805 then 0.746), so the fit is smoothing noise as
    well as trend.
  * ONE DOCUMENT.
  * NOT ADOPTED. `formularesidual` still uses the single floor. The
    harness is committed and the decision is not mine: it turns over
    two-thirds of the red set and buys no source detection, so it is
    a change in what the report is ABOUT.
