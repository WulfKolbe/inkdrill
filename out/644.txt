THE REVIEWER'S DIAGNOSES, CONFIRMED NUMERICALLY

  FO0237 "several characters to the right"  -- the refit moves it
         +260 px right and the blob count becomes EXACT, 37 against
         37, score 0.581 -> 0.709. Confirmed precisely.

  FO0200 "the rightmost Fraktur glyph was missed" -- the right edge
         extends by 52 px and picks it up: 39 against 39, score
         0.629 -> 0.831. Confirmed precisely.

  FO0207 "large miss in size and position" -- the refit moves the
         rect 369 px, which is the largest correction in the set, and
         recovers 5 of the 6 missing blobs. Confirmed as misplacement;
         not fully resolved.

  FO0236/FO0237 OVERLAP was RIGHT and it identified the right one.
         The pair overlapped because FO0237 sat 260 px too far left,
         inside FO0236's rectangle. FO0236 barely moves. A rule with
         one observation behind it got that observation correct.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
AND ONE DIAGNOSIS OF MINE THAT WAS WRONG

I read FO0150's image as an under-scaled rectangle -- `11` missing on
the left, `)` on the right, 3 blobs, exactly the 53->50 gap. A scale
error explains that shape, so I expected the refit to close it.

IT DOES NOT. FO0150 moves 2 px and stays at 53->50.

The reason is structural, and it is a better finding than the one I
guessed. The expression is `11 R(x y)=...`: MathPix has folded the
EQUATION NUMBER into the formula. On the page that number is set flush
left with a wide gap before the maths; in `$11 R(x y)$` it is one thin
math space. No scale reconciles the two, because the difference is not
a scale -- the rendered expression is a different SHAPE from the ink.

FO0144 (`7 \quad a(\bar{a} x)...`) and FO0154 (`12.2 \quad a_{i}...`)
have the same form. That is a class worth naming, and it accuses the
SOURCE: an equation number pulled into the expression it labels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE FIFTEEN ACTUALLY ARE

At least three causes, where out/643 implied one:

  A. MISPLACEMENT, curable by a per-row refit.
     FO0200, FO0237 outright; FO0207 substantially.
  B. AN EQUATION NUMBER ABSORBED INTO THE EXPRESSION, which no
     placement can fix because the shapes differ.
     FO0150, and by inspection FO0144 and FO0154 share the form.
  C. STILL OPEN. Seven rows have an EXACT blob count and improve only
     modestly under the refit -- FO0096 0.71->0.83, FO0111
     0.71->0.74, FO0170 0.65->0.69, FO0228 0.68->0.69. Neither
     misplacement nor a count difference explains those.
