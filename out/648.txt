648 - MathPix's LaTeX against the author's, over 389 documents
===============================================================

out/647 used one author source to settle one token. Most arXiv papers
ship theirs, so the same comparison runs corpus-wide with no
rasteriser, no scale estimate and no placement in between -- three
things that have each been wrong at least once in 639-646.

`tools/authordiff.py census` and `... geometry`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS AVAILABLE

    2,826 document directories
      389  have BOTH an author `.tgz` and MathPix output
      500  have an author `.tgz` at all
        7  have an author source AND an evidence table

THE FILE EXTENSION LIES ABOUT THE FORMAT. 29 of the 389 are a gzip of
ONE `.tex`; the other 360 are real tarballs, from 1 to 190 `.tex`
members. The reader decides on the ustar magic, not the name, and
concatenates every member -- a paper split across files would
otherwise be read as whichever happens to be first.

AND THE HALF THAT MAKES THE CENSUS USEFUL

A RATIO SAYS MATHPIX WRITES SOMETHING ELSE. IT DOES NOT SAY THE PAGE
LOOKS DIFFERENT. Each pair rendered and the ink compared:

    class                mathpix  author   delta       verdict
    { }^{ } prescript        475     436    +39  (+8.9%)  GEOMETRIC
    \left(                   302     288    +14  (+4.9%)  GEOMETRIC
    \mid                     675     657    +18  (+2.7%)  GEOMETRIC
    \operatorname            334     334     +0            identical ink
    \left\{                  286     286     +0            identical ink
    \cdots                   352     352     +0            identical ink
    \boldsymbol              107     107     +0   same width, other glyphs

`\operatorname{det}` FOR `\det` IS THE MOST SUBSTITUTED TOKEN IN THE
CORPUS -- 23x, 257 documents where the author never writes it -- AND
IT RENDERS TO IDENTICAL INK. It cannot produce a residual and must
never be flagged as one. Same for `\left\{` at this size and `\cdots`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RANKING THIS PRODUCES, AND IT IS NOT THE ONE I WAS WORKING ON

Geometric effect against prevalence:

  1. `{ }^{ }` PRESCRIPT   +8.9% width, introduced in 320 of 333
     documents, 88x the author rate. The dominant class by both
     measures, and by a distance.
  2. `\left(`              +4.9%, 176 documents.
  3. `\mid`                +2.7%, 113 documents.
  4. `\boldsymbol`         a GLYPH substitution rather than a spacing
     one -- same width, different ink -- 84 documents. A different
     kind of defect and it needs a different check.

I SPENT out/646 AND out/647 ON THE THIRD ONE. `\mid` surfaced because
it was enriched among eighteen flagged rows, and eighteen flagged rows
are the rows that FAILED, not a census. The corpus says the empty-group
prescript is three times the effect in three times the documents, and
nothing in the red list pointed at it.

That is the same shape as every population finding this project has
recorded: the instrument was right and the sample it was reasoning
from was not the population.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT MEANS FOR AN EDGE-CASE COLLECTION

1,637 substituted bars across 113 documents is not 1,637 findings; it
is one finding with 1,637 instances. A collection that keeps them all
is 1,637 copies of the same edge, and a collection that keeps four
representatives of each CLASS covers more ground in less space. The
census gives the classes and the geometry test says which of them can
produce a visible residual at all -- three of the seven tested.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT CLAIMED

  * NOT AN ALIGNED COMPARISON. Token counts over whole documents
    cannot say a given expression was changed, only that one source
    uses a construct the other does not. Alignment needs the evidence
    table and only 7 of 389 pairs have one.
  * THE GEOMETRY IS ONE MINIMAL EXPRESSION PER CLASS. `\left(` around
    a fraction differs far more than `\left(x+y\right)`; the deltas
    are a floor, not an average.
  * `\boldsymbol{R}` was paired against `\mathbf{R}`. The author of
    0902.0431 writes a macro `\R`, and what it expands to was not
    checked, so that row is the weakest in the table.
  * 389 of 2,826 directories. Whether documents WITH a source differ
    from those without is unmeasured.
  * Nothing was changed in the pipeline.
