640 - the residual report: which evidence rows are WRONG
=========================================================

REPORT ONLY. `tools/formularesidual.py` consumes `formulafind.py`'s
JSON. The ask was to invert 639: instead of placing every expression,
find the rows where something has gone wrong, mark those, and later
show only those.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DESIGN DECISION THAT MATTERS

NOT PLACING A ROW IS NOT EVIDENCE OF ANYTHING.

639 measured that 49 of 200 rows carry one gap or none -- `G`, `w`,
`\mathfrak{D}^{\prime}` -- and that an expression that short has no
position to find. Marking those red would publish the instrument's
blind spot as the document's error. That is the failure CLAUDE.md
records as "a filter that excludes the class it exists to compare
against", and it would poison the residual report with its largest
class.

So there are THREE outcomes, not two, and the middle one is the one
that is usually got wrong:

    SILENT   placed, and every check agrees
    GREY     NOT EXAMINED -- the expression is too short to carry a
             position, or the line repeats and the position is not
             established. Reported as not examined. NEVER as wrong.
    RED      CONTRADICTED -- two computations that share no code
             disagree.

Measured over 260 rows of 0902.0431:

    SILENT        81   31.2%
    GREY         163   62.7%   (61 unplaceable, 102 ambiguous)
    RED           16    6.2%   <- the residual report

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIVE RED RULES, AND WHAT EACH ACCUSES

  R1 EDGE_CUT    a rectangle boundary falls INSIDE a blob. A correct
                 placement ends in a gap, because that is what a gap
                 is. Accuses the placement.            fired on 1
  R2 BLOB_COUNT  the rendered LaTeX has a different component count
                 from the ink it sits on. Accuses the SOURCE -- a
                 missing subscript, a swallowed operator.  fired on 8
  R3 POOR_FIT    the position is established (high margin: it fits
                 here and nowhere else) and yet the shape agrees
                 badly. Where it is versus what it looks like.
                                                        fired on 9
  R4 OVERLAP     two expressions on ONE line placed on overlapping x.
                 Impossible.                            fired on 0
  R5 ORDER       two expressions on one line placed out of reading
                 order. Impossible.                     fired on 0

EVERY THRESHOLD IS CALIBRATED FROM THE ROWS THAT AGREE, not chosen.
The reference population is the 97 confidently placed rows; the cut is
their 90th percentile (10th for the score), so about a tenth of the
reference set would flag itself. That is stated rather than hidden: a
rule that fires on nothing it was calibrated against has no measured
false-positive rate at all.

    blob_diff_max  2     spread {0:63, 1:17, 2:9, 3:5, 5:1, 7:1, 13:1}
    edge_cut_max   2     spread {0:30, 1:50, 2:16, 3:1}
    score_min      0.6698

R4 AND R5 NEED NO EXTERNAL DATA. Rows on the same line are given
BYTE-IDENTICAL copies of the crop, so the content hash recovers the
line: 183 distinct crops among 260 rows, 56 shared by more than one.

  THE FIRST VERSION GROUPED BY PATH AND FOUND 0 SHARED LINES, which
  silently disabled both line rules and reported them as firing on
  nothing. A rule that cannot fire and a rule that finds nothing print
  the same zero.

  THE READING-ORDER PREMISE IS VERIFIED BEFORE IT IS USED TO ACCUSE:
  on the 9 pairs where both rows are confidently placed, x order
  matches id order 9 times out of 9.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE RULE WAS RETRACTED, AND IT IS THE MOST USEFUL THING HERE

R3 was first written as SCALE: flag a row whose own best-fit scale is
far from the document's, reasoning that a different length accuses the
LaTeX. It fired on 9 rows and looked convincing.

ALL 9 WERE ARTEFACTS OF MY OWN SEARCH. The own-scale distribution over
the 97 reference rows is bimodal:

    0.24-0.27   85 rows      <- the document's true scale
    0.10-0.15   10 rows      <- the FLOOR of the search range

`FO0036` scored a PERFECT 1.000 at scale 0.100 -- the same degeneracy
639 found in `G`: a template shrunk until it fits anywhere. The rule
was measuring the instrument, and every row it accused was a row where
the free-scale search had collapsed.

What those rows actually have in common is something better: at the
DOCUMENT scale their margin is high and their score is low. FO0039
scores 0.633 with a margin of 0.633 -- the runner-up scored zero. It
fits in exactly one place, and badly. That is a contradiction between
two computations rather than an artefact of one, so R3 became POOR_FIT
and is calibrated on the score distribution.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RED ROWS ARE REAL. THREE CHECKED BY EYE

    FO0045  conf 1.000  BLOB_COUNT+POOR_FIT  S U(3)) / \boldsymbol{Z}_{3}
            The crop is 159x22 and shows "= {a". THE EXPRESSION IS
            NOT IN IT AT ALL. Note also the doubled paren in the
            LaTeX itself.

    FO0059  conf 1.000  BLOB_COUNT+POOR_FIT  (S U(3) \times S U(6)) / ...
            The crop is 635x24 and is essentially BLANK -- the only
            ink found is a sliver at y 1-3. A row whose evidence
            contains no evidence.

    FO0039  conf 1.000  POOR_FIT             z\left(E_{6}\right)
            The crop reads roughly "(E_6)_0" where the LaTeX says
            "z(E_6)". A genuine disagreement about what is there.

15 OF THE 16 RED ROWS CARRY A SOURCE CONFIDENCE >= 0.99.

That is the whole point of the package. Column 3 already holds the
other tool's opinion of itself, and it says 1.000 on a row whose crop
is blank. The finding is not either number, it is the DISAGREEMENT --
which is why the mark belongs beside that cell rather than replacing
it, and why sorting the residual report by source confidence
descending puts the most valuable rows first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT SHOULD LOOK

  * In the full table: a RED rectangle drawn on the col-6 crop at the
    located position, and a red mark beside the col-3 confidence.
    GREY rows get a grey mark meaning NOT EXAMINED, with no rectangle
    -- there is nothing to draw and drawing a guess would be worse
    than drawing nothing. Silent rows are left alone.
  * The residuals report is the red rows only, sorted by source
    confidence descending, each with its flag names, its rectangle,
    and the two numbers that disagreed. 16 rows out of 260 is a page,
    not a report to skim.
  * The GREY count must be printed at the top of that report. A
    residual report that shows 16 rows without saying that 163 were
    never examined invites the reader to conclude the other 244 are
    fine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT CLAIMED

  * ONE DOCUMENT, 260 of 3,163 rows. 0902.0431 only.
  * R4 and R5 FIRED ON NOTHING. They are sound and cost nothing, but
    9 comparable pairs is not a test of them. They are unproven, not
    clean.
  * POOR_FIT AND BLOB_COUNT CANNOT YET SEPARATE "the LaTeX is wrong"
    from "the crop is wrong" from "my renderer differs from the
    paper's font". FO0045 and FO0059 are plainly crop failures;
    FO0039 might be either. The classes are worth splitting and the
    evidence to do it -- the ink that is NOT covered by any placed
    expression -- has not been used.
  * NO GROUND TRUTH, so there is no measured false-negative rate.
    Three red rows were confirmed by eye; nothing says how many wrong
    rows sit in the 81 SILENT ones.
