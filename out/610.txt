610 - centrelines in the spec, and the direct comparison re-run
====================================================================

STATED. `rule_above_bp`, `rule_below_bp` and `column_rules_bp` are
CENTRELINES, in out/601.txt and in `cellrect`'s G2. The consumer
insets by `rule_width_bp / 2` on every side, which it has done since
602 and which is now pinned by a test that checks the arithmetic
rather than a remembered number -- an emitter sending an edge instead
is the one thing that property cannot survive.

THE DIRECT COMPARISON IS NOT 0.00. The manifest on disk is the one 608
measured, unchanged (same mtime, same values, sha still MATCH), so
this re-run says what remains rather than what was fixed.

    0049, no raster and no lattice: manifest bp against the PDF's own
    stroke paths, both centrelines

    COLUMN RULES (x)   n 17   median +6.1770   min +6.1760  max +6.1770
    ROW RULES    (y)   n 66   median +0.1990   min -0.1990  max +0.1990

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE y OFFSET IS EXACTLY HALF A RULE, WHICH IS THE THING 610 NAMES

    half of the declared rule_width_bp 0.4      0.2000
    half of the ACTUAL stroke width 0.3985      0.19925
    the measured offset                         0.1990

    rule_above_bp   +0.199 on 32 of 33 rows,  -0.199 on one
    rule_below_bp   +0.199 on 33 of 33

Every one of the 66 values is exactly one half-rule from a real rule
centreline. So the manifest is emitting EDGES today, not centrelines,
and the sign says which edge: the upper one, on 65 of 66. The single
exception is `0049_DIA_0001` on page 3, which takes the lower edge of
its rule -- so it is not even a consistent edge convention.

That is the whole of the y disagreement. With centrelines emitted it
goes to 0.000 by construction, because the consumer already derives
the edges from `rule_width_bp`.

VERIFIED THAT THE PDF SIDE IS THE CENTRELINE, since the claim rests on
it. On page 1 the PDF's top rule is 692.852 bp; at 300 dpi that
predicts ink spanning raster rows 620.16..621.82, and the ink is on
rows 620 and 621, nothing above or below:

    y 619:   0 px
    y 620: 250 px   <-- rule
    y 621: 250 px   <-- rule
    y 622:   0 px

The manifest's 693.051 would predict rows 619.33..620.99 -- one row
too high, which is what an upper edge read as a centreline gives.

2. THE x OFFSET IS NOT A CENTRELINE QUESTION

    +6.177 bp on all 17 column rules, across all three tables

Half a rule is 0.199. 6.177 is thirty-one times that, it is constant
rather than proportional, and it does not change sign. It is the
origin offset 608 reported and it is untouched by this decision --
emitting centrelines will not move it.

3. WHAT THE RE-RUN CHANGES ABOUT 608's LIST

608 asked for three fixes. This one refines the third and adds
nothing:

    1. the +6.177 bp x origin                       UNCHANGED
    2. the missing rightmost column rule            UNCHANGED
    3. the first row's rule_above on each page      UNCHANGED
    4. NEW: emit the rule CENTRELINE, not an edge -- +0.199 bp on
       every row rule, and inconsistent in sign on one of them

Item 4 is worth 1.66 px at 300 dpi, which is smaller than the other
two and is the only one the consumer could have absorbed silently. It
could not be absorbed by INSPECTION, though: an edge is a plausible
centreline, and it was findable only by comparing against a third
source that was known to be a centreline.
