672 - "(not rendered)" read as maths, and 15 refinements that no longer compile
=============================================================================

pdfdrill's 696 bracket repair changed four formula readings (mielke
FO0901, FO1309; johnston FO1528; cardona FO0284). `formulamarks update`
on mielke and johnston met out/671's rule -- every row not updated
identical to pdfdrill's freshly built marks, scale and calibration equal
-- but johnston's two updated rows were wrong in a way that rule cannot
see.

====================================================================
1. THE PLACEHOLDER INSIDE \FitMath
====================================================================

johnston FO5033 and FO1528 print `\FitMath{\emph{(not rendered)}}`.
`rows()` took any `\FitMath` content as the reading, so `update` looked
the rows' host lines up by MathPix's reading and then RENDERED THE WORDS
"(not rendered)" and measured them as a formula. FO5033 became a
measured, unmarked record of that text; FO1528 "not placed".

Every `\FitMath` in the 21 published documents, 2026-09-15:

    $\displaystyle ...$         45,371
    \emph{(not rendered)}           33   (10 formula rows, 23 equation rows)

THE FIX: only `$...$` is a reading; anything else leaves `math` None and
is kept as `placeholder`. `marks` now reports a row with no reading as
"not rendered by the producer" BEFORE looking for a measured record, so
a leftover record cannot surface as "reading changed". Tests: TF_1 (a
placeholder row, and a `$...$` row untouched), TF_5 (a placeholder row
with a leftover update record). Suite 1,278, 37 skip.

WHAT IT HAD COST. Re-emitting every document's marks from its stored
work after the fix: NO mark count changed anywhere and no marked row
moved. Across both my files and pdfdrill's built ones, exactly one
placeholder row had ever been measured (johnston FO5033, today's update)
and none marked. The only differences are placeholder rows changing
their stated reason:

    fong-spivak-invitation  FO1478, FO1481        "no line match" -> not rendered
    fong-spivak-seven-sk.   FO1471, FO1476, FO1478
    kohlhase-omdoc          FO0004, FO0399, FO0400
    johnston                FO1528 ("not placed"), FO5033 (measured) -> not rendered
    cardona                 FO0284 (09-13 record)  -> not rendered   (NOT placed:
                                                     pdfdrill is rebuilding cardona)

Placed for the four documents whose only change is placeholder rows; each
previous file kept as marks-before-672.json. gilmore EQ0474 -- out/668's
one candidate with no host line -- is one of the 23 equation rows.

====================================================================
2. WHY THOSE ROWS SAY "(not rendered)": 15 REFINEMENTS DO NOT COMPILE
====================================================================

johnston FO1528 and cardona FO0284 are two of the 696 repair's own rows.
Compiling every `latex_refined` in the 21 documents' tiddlers.json with
formulafind's standalone preamble:

    refined readings                          136
    do not compile                             18
      MathPix's `latex` compiles (a REGRESSION,
      all census-verified)                     15
      MathPix's `latex` does not compile either
      (ink-verified, pre-existing)              3   0902.0431 EQ1032,
                                                    mielke EQ0972, johnston EQ1276

The 15 regressions:

    0902.0431   EQ0594, EQ0704, EQ0946, EQ1091, EQ1095, EQ1211
    cardona     FO0284, EQ0066
    mielke      EQ0067, EQ0605, EQ0954
    johnston    FO1528, EQ0909, EQ0936
    gilmore     EQ0444

The shapes visible in the readings. TeX pairs `\left`/`\right` only
within one group and one alignment cell, and a per-type counter cannot
see either:

  * promoted across an alignment `&`:
    `k_J^{-1}\left( & A(...)\right)`  (0902.0431 EQ0594, EQ0704, EQ1091, EQ1095)
  * promoted INSIDE a brace group whose `\right` is outside it:
    `\mathbb{Z}^{\left( } \mathbb{R}^d\right)` (cardona FO0284),
    `{\left[11} \\ 22 ...\right]` (johnston FO1528), mielke EQ0605, EQ0954
  * across `\overbrace`/`\underbrace` groups (johnston EQ0909, EQ0936)
  * `\cancel{\right)}` -- a closer moved into a group (gilmore EQ0444)

MathPix's `\left. ... \right]` in these rows is typographically wrong
but COMPILES; the repair makes it right-looking and uncompilable, and
the evidence shows the placeholder instead of either.

====================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
====================================================================

  * Compilation is with formulafind's preamble (amsmath, amssymb,
    stmaryrd, cancel, ...), not pdfdrill's `display_safe`; a reading
    could compile in one and not the other. The evidence placeholder on
    FO1528 and FO0284 agrees with it here.
  * The shapes of EQ0946, EQ1211, EQ0066 and EQ0067 were not read
    (readings truncated in the listing).
  * Nothing here decides the fix. The repair and its gate are
    pdfdrill's.

    cardona      1,068, the same; FO0284 back on MathPix's reading is its
                 09-13 record again, update 0
    johnston     1,586; FO1528 needed the fix below

A SECOND GAP IN `update`, found by reading FO1528 through before running
it: an earlier update had attempted the row (then a placeholder) and left
no record. `merge` drops an attempted row's original record, and `update`
skipped every attempted row without a record, so once the reading came
back FO1528 would have kept "not placed" forever -- which is exactly what
pdfdrill's build showed. Now an attempted row with no record and a real
reading is attempted again. Test: TF_5. Suite 1,279, 37 skip.

After the fix FO1528 measures as its 09-13 record did (unmarked: host
region is not a line); the other 5,041 rows identical; placed.
