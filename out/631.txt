THE MEASUREMENT REPRODUCES ON THE MERGE

Same harness, same population -- the 42 DISTINCT pages of
kolbe2018hubbard/inspect/pages, 649.6 Mpx. Best of 3, gc.collect()
between runs, both implementations timed in both orders.

    capture  order          reference     sweep    change
    none     ref first        10.626s    7.163s    -32.6%
    none     sweep first      10.638s    7.191s    -32.4%
    graph    ref first        12.322s   11.168s     -9.4%
    graph    sweep first      12.325s   11.158s     -9.5%

    none  mean: 10.632s -> 7.177s = -32.5%  (1.48x)
    graph mean: 12.323s -> 11.163s = -9.4%  (1.10x)

Against the branch's 630 figures of -32.5% and -9.5%, so the merge
costs nothing. The orders agree to a decimal within each row, which is
what says there is no warm-cache effect being reported as a gain.

POPULATION AND SPLIT RULE. 42 pages of one document, one corpus,
LaTeX-produced. No split: this is two implementations timed on
identical input, not a fit, so there is nothing to hold out. The
number that would generalise less well is the RATIO between the two
capture levels, which depends on how much of a page is ink.

WHAT IS STILL OPEN

The CR named three and all three remain open:

  1. Whether the step-1 gain survives at Capture.GRAPH. The -9.4%
     above is the WHOLE BRANCH against the reference, not step 1, and
     step 1 was never measured at GRAPH on its own.
  2. Whether the node-id invariant G8 holds for a future SweepResult
     producer. The test can only check the two that exist -- sweep and
     band.sweep_banded -- and G8 is now load bearing at seven sites.
  3. The memory cost of component_of's index under a corpus run
     holding many results open. Unmeasured.

And one the CR did not name, found while running it:

  4. version._head_of cannot resolve a linked worktree's symbolic
     HEAD, because a worktree's gitdir has an empty refs/ and the
     branch ref lives in the common dir. Pre-existing -- proved by
     building a worktree on a branch at f3fc189, before the CR and
     before the bundle. A detached-HEAD worktree passes. The fix is
     to follow `commondir`; not done, out of the CR's scope. It
     surfaces as test_the_version_matches_the_checkout asserting
     'unknown' != 'unknown', which reads like a logic bug and is not.
