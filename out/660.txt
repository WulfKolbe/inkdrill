====================================================================
4. LIMIT ONE: `margin` IS BLIND WHEN AN EXPRESSION FILLS ITS LINE
====================================================================

margin = best score - best NON-OVERLAPPING rival. A rival must start
at least one template width away, so when the template is wider than
half its line there is no room for one: the rival is 0 and margin
equals score. The MARK_MARGIN clause cannot fire on such a row at
all.

    marked rows with no possible rival   3,010 of 8,453 (35.6%)

Johnston FO3893, `x_n = x_{n-1} - 4x_{n-2} + 4x_{n-3}`: the refit
shrank the rendering to 0.499 (the line's 703 px leave no room for a
506 px rival), offset 134 scored 0.716 against 0.622 at the true
start, and the mark went on "n-1 ... when n", margin 0.716.

How risky the class is, is NOT established. Among the 155 eye
verdicts of 653-657 only 31 rows are ones the policy marks (the review
sampled flagged rows, which the policy suppresses); 8 of those have no
possible rival and all 8 are right. Eight rows prove nothing about a
class this size -- they only show it is not always wrong.

code. Fixed in 210004f (lenient decode; a crash is refused as a
crash; both refusals tested). Re-run: scale 0.64, 185 of 1,793 rows marked, 1,790 measured.
