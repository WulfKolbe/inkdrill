196 - ink.rules[] inside a matrix bbox
========================================================================

ANSWER: ink.rules[] reports ZERO rules inside the matrix bbox. The
LaTeX says there are SIX. Both the count and the reason are below,
and the reason is not the one 116 found.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE PAGE, AND HOW IT WAS CHOSEN

The task named a document but not a page, so the page was selected by
GROUND TRUTH rather than by eye: the report's own tex was searched for
equation rows whose LaTeX contains a ruled array. Eleven exist in this
document; EQ0192 has the most rule structure of any of them.

  document  Geometrodynamics of Gauge Fields ... (Mielke) (Z-Library)
  row       ...__EQ0192,  source page 081
  region    MathPix `math` line, top_left (379, 1260), 763 x 99,
            on a 1526 x 2313 page
  render    source page 81 at 300 dpi -> 1831 x 2775, scale 1.1999
  bbox      (454, 1511) - (1370, 1630) in page px, 916 x 119

The LaTeX, which is the ground truth for what should be found:

    a:=\left[\begin{array}{c|c} g & \mathrm{a} \\ \hline 0 & 1
      \end{array}\right]
     =\left[\begin{array}{c|c} \mathbb{1}_{n} & \mathrm{a} \\ \hline
      0 & 1 \end{array}\right]
      \left[\begin{array}{c|c} g & 0 \\ \hline 0 & 1
      \end{array}\right]
     = a^{\mathrm{T}} \cdot a^{\mathrm{L}} \in A(n,\mathbb{R}).

THREE arrays, each `{c|c}` with one `\hline`. Expected: 3 vertical
rules and 3 horizontal rules, six in total.

    ink regions inside the bbox        44

Zero of six.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHERE THE SIX RULES ACTUALLY ARE

They are present in the ink and they are found -- as three components,
not six, and none of them shaped like a rule. Three regions in the
bbox have a fill of 0.06:

    region        fill    vertical band        horizontal band
    82 x 103     0.065    x-offset 40-42       y-offset 50-52
                          103 of 103 px tall   82 of 82 px wide
    103 x 103    0.057    x-offset 61-63       y-offset 50-52
                          103 of 103 px        103 of 103 px
    82 x 103     0.065    x-offset 39-41       y-offset 50-52
                          103 of 103 px        82 of 82 px

Each is a CROSS. A 3 px vertical band spanning the region's full
height, and a 3 px horizontal band spanning its full width, meeting
in the middle. That is exactly `{c|c}` plus `\hline`: the column rule
and the row rule INTERSECT, so 8-connectivity merges them into one
component per matrix.

POSITION RELATIVE TO THE CELL GRID, which is what the task asked for
and which the geometry gives directly:

    matrix   vertical rule at   horizontal rule at   reading
    1        0.50 of width      0.50 of height       {c|c} + \hline
    2        0.60 of width      0.50 of height       {c|c} + \hline
    3        0.49 of width      0.50 of height       {c|c} + \hline

Every vertical sits between column 1 and column 2; every horizontal
between row 1 and row 2. A 2x2 grid divided once each way, three
times over. The structure the task set out to read is unambiguously
there in the ink.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHY `is_rule` REFUSES THEM -- A DIFFERENT REASON FROM 116

`is_rule` requires fill > 0.8 AND a long side >= 20x the short one.
A cross fails BOTH, and not marginally:

    fill    0.06   against a floor of 0.80
    aspect  ~1.0   against a floor of 20

116 found the aspect gate emptying the population on scanned pages --
205 solid bars at aspect 10-20 against 3 at >= 20 -- and left open
what those bars were. This is a DIFFERENT failure and it matters that
they are not confused:

  116  a real bar, correctly shaped, too THICK for the aspect gate
  196  a real rule that is not a bar at all, because it is fused to
       the rule it crosses

Loosening the aspect constant would not recover a single rule here.
The component is not rule-shaped; it is two rules and a junction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE NEAR-MISSES ARE PUNCTUATION

The only solid bars in the bbox are six horizontal ones in three
pairs, fill 1.00, aspect 9.3 and 14.0, at x 52-79, 235-262, 571-598:

    y-offset 57-58, 28 x 2 px      aspect 14.0
    y-offset 65-67, 28 x 3 px      aspect  9.3

Two bars, stacked, 7 px apart, three times. Those are the `:=` and
the two `=` signs. They are correctly absent from ink.rules[] -- an
equals sign is not a rule -- but they are the only thing in this bbox
that a loosened aspect gate would admit. Widening the constant on the
strength of 116 would, on this page, add three equals signs and no
matrix rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT WOULD BE NEEDED, NOT BUILT

Detecting these needs a component SPLIT, not a threshold change: a
low-fill component whose column profile has one full-height band and
whose row profile has one full-width band is a rule crossing, and the
two bands are the two rules. The profiles above were computed exactly
that way and separated all three cases cleanly on the first attempt.

That is a change to what `is_rule` is, not to its constants, and it
needs its own premise measurement -- how often a crossing occurs, and
what else has a full-span band in one axis -- before it is built. 116
left an open question about what the aspect 10-20 population contains;
this adds a second, and both should be answered by one measurement
rather than two guesses.
