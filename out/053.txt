==========================================================================

time; the report prints \emph{(not rendered)} in its Rendered cell.
Its ink is 13 components and 6 holes WHATEVER the equation was, so
comparing it against a scan of real mathematics measures the
placeholder, not the conversion. Such a row has no rendering to
compare and is now excluded with the reason named, exactly as the
row/equation mismatch already is.

model: the model-side label missed 2 of the 4 demoted rows in
