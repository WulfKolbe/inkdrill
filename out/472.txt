472 - extent, aspect and ink-per-max: computed, reported, not gating
========================================================================

THREE SIBLINGS, per cell, for the render and the scan:

    extent        the cell's ink bounding box, w x h px
    aspect        that box's w/h
    ink_per_max   ink pixels / max(w,h) -- what the longest side is
                  carrying, which separates a long thin mark from a
                  dense block of the same extent

Nothing gates on any of them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THREE POPULATION FACTS, BEFORE ANY NUMBER

5,144 IS NOT A ROW COUNT ON THIS SIDE. It is the FO count from
pdfdrill out/460. A render-versus-scan shape comparison needs a cell
of each, which only the display rows have:

    FO objects (pdfdrill out/460)        5,144
    \ident{...EQ} rows in report.tex     1,670
    crops on disk                        1,713
    rows measured here                   1,936

Reporting a distribution "over 5,144 rows" would be quoting their
object count as my row count -- the 157-against-49 mismatch again.

THE ROWS OUTNUMBER THE EQUATIONS, 1,936 against 1,670, and that is
the population problem. johnston declares THREE longtables and TWO of
them are 6-column. The probe found 283 of 284 pages at 6 columns and
took all of them, so the measured rows span both tables. This is not
the display-equation population alone, and positional pairing to
identifiers is therefore REFUSED (238's rule: rows must equal
identifiers exactly, not merely be fewer).

REPORT.PDF IS A READING BUILD -- phase=reading, legend=true,
ink_adopted=true, built 2026-09-01T13:48:11Z. My own guard refuses
these for residual measurement. Proceeding here deliberately, because
472 is an internal shape question rather than a publication
measurement and the legend adds rows without altering the equation
cells -- but it is the artefact class that was wrong twice for
pdfdrill, so it is on the record.

1. THE DISTRIBUTIONS, 1,936 ROWS

    render / scan          p5     p25     p50     p75     p90     p95     p99    max
    aspect ratio        0.944   1.053   1.147   1.277   1.456   1.540   7.630  22.289
    ink_per_max         0.541   0.644   0.741   0.873   0.999   1.025   1.109   1.906
    extent area         0.750   1.000   1.094   1.388   1.655   1.781   2.018   2.647

ASPECT IS THE ONE WITH A TAIL. Its body is tight -- p5 to p95 spans
0.94 to 1.54, a factor of 1.6 -- and then p99 is 7.63 and the maximum
22.3. Between p95 and p99 the ratio jumps five-fold. The other two
have no such break: ink_per_max runs 0.54 to 1.03 across the same
range and extent area 0.75 to 1.78.

That shape -- a tight body and a thin far tail -- is what a shape
DISAGREEMENT would look like if it existed as a distinct class. It is
not proof that it does. A long tail is also what a handful of
mis-cropped or mis-detected cells produce, and section 0 says the
population contains rows from two different tables.

Measured from its own named crop file, which is unambiguous, rather

    latex     \overbrace{(1,2) /}^{\substack{y \\ C}} x
    crop      extent 225 x 281,  aspect 0.801,  ink_per_max 13.08,

ITS ASPECT IS AT THE 0.6TH PERCENTILE -- taller relative to its width
than 99.4% of the scan cells measured. That is the shape the LaTeX
describes: a `\substack` of two lines set above an `\overbrace` makes
a crop far taller than a line of maths.

Its ink_per_max is unremarkable, 69th percentile. So of the three
siblings, one places it at the extreme and two do not.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, AND WHY NO THRESHOLD IS FITTED

One example at the 0.6th percentile is one example. 246 refused a
rule that put one diagram above the 94th percentile on three
statistics against 75 equations; 305 fitted three populations before
finding the normaliser was wrong, and its stated mechanism turned out
to be a tautology. The pattern in both was the same -- a number that
looks decisive on the case that motivated it.

What would make aspect a discriminator is a LABELLED SET: rows known
to disagree in shape, and rows known not to, chosen before the
statistic is computed. Then the p95-to-p99 jump either separates them
or does not. Until that exists, the tail is a shape in a
distribution and EQ1327 is a point in it.

The measurement stands on its own regardless: the three siblings are
computed and reported per cell, which is what the task asked for, and
they are available to any later gate without one being built now.
