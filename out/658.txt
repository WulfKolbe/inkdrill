658 - CLOSURE on marking, and the four workstreams separated
=============================================================

Four things have been tangled together in 639-657. They are separate
problems with separate evidence and separate owners.

====================================================================
1. MARKING INLINE MATH -- CLOSED
====================================================================

THE POLICY. A rectangle is drawn on an evidence row only when

    gaps >= 2                    the expression carries a position
    margin >= 0.15               it fits here and not elsewhere
    NOT (gaps <= 7 AND cuts >= 1) no boundary lands inside a glyph
    the crop is line-like        crop height <= 3x the page median

Implemented in `tools/formularesidual.py` as MARK_MARGIN / MARK_GAPS /
MARK_CUTS with the pricing table beside them.

  CORRECTION, 2026-09-11. When this file was written the FOURTH clause
  -- the line-like crop -- was applied by an external filter and was
  NOT in the tool, so "implemented" was true of three clauses out of
  four. A caller running `formularesidual` alone would have marked
  rows sitting on page-sized regions. It is now in the tool as
  NON_LINE = 3, beside the other three, and reproduces the external
  filter exactly: 1,474 of 3,163 rows marked either way, 0 of the 67
  non-line rows marked.

WHAT IT COSTS AND BUYS, priced on 2,240 placed rows carrying an
estimated 20 real placement errors (out/654, out/655), against 155 eye
verdicts:

    policy                          marks   wrong marks   a mark is right
    margin>=0.10, gaps<=2 & cuts>=2  99.8%       ~13.3          99.40%
    margin>=0.15, gaps<=5 & cuts>=2  79.5%        ~5.0          99.72%
    CHOSEN                           66.2%        ~1.7          99.89%
    margin>=0.20, gaps<=7 & cuts>=2  58.1%        ~3.3          99.74%

The last is DOMINATED -- fewer marks and more errors -- so the real
choice was the middle two.

A THIRD OF EXPRESSIONS LOSE THEIR RECTANGLE TO TAKE THE EXPECTED WRONG
MARKS FROM THIRTEEN TO UNDER TWO. For a published corpus that is the
right way round. A mark in the wrong place sends a reader to the wrong
glyphs and looks authoritative doing it; a missing mark costs only
that the reader finds the expression themselves, which is what they do
today.

On 0902.0431: 1,474 of 3,096 rows marked, 1,622 suppressed.

THE EVIDENCE BEHIND EACH CLAUSE
  gaps >= 2      0 of 49 one-gap rows could be placed at all (out/639)
  margin         the score cannot gate: at short lengths the WRONG
                 rows score HIGHER than the right ones (out/653)
  cuts           of 12 labelled errors every one had a boundary
                 inside a glyph; 37 rows with none were all correct
  line-like      67 rows sat on page-sized regions and scored 0.94
                 for nonsense (out/651)

====================================================================
2. DETECTING MATHPIX ERRORS -- OPEN, AND NOTHING FOUND YET
====================================================================

155 eye verdicts, 12 errors, ALL TWELVE INKDRILL'S OWN PLACEMENT. Not
one recognition error has been found by this instrument.

That is not evidence that there are none. The instrument selects rows
by PLACEMENT FIT, so a recognition error that placed cleanly never
reached a reviewer. It has never sampled recognition at all.

WHAT THE RESIDUAL REPORT SHOULD CARRY, and what exists today:

  * LOW SOURCE CONFIDENCE -- implemented, conf < 0.80. 161 rows on
    0902.0431 (5.2%), 103 on 1510.06699 (9.7%).
  * CORRELATION AND PLAUSIBILITY GAPS -- not built. The signals that
    exist and are unused: blob count of the rendered expression
    against the ink inside its own rectangle; the width the LaTeX
    predicts against the width measured; ink inside the rectangle
    that no rendered glyph covers.
  * The RED flags are NOT this. They are evidence-table marks for
    someone chasing a detail, and the tool now says so.

====================================================================
3. REPAIRING INLINE MATH WHITESPACE -- NOT OURS, MEASURED FOR HANDOVER
====================================================================

The measurement is the deliverable. out/656 and out/657:

  * MathPix inserts ~30% more intra-token spaces than the author, and
    the rate is FLAT with expression length -- 1.38x, 1.34x, 1.26x,
    1.32x per 100 characters across four length bands. It does not
    concentrate in large expressions; it accumulates in them.
  * SIX OF EIGHT tested differences are INVISIBLE. `x y` and `xy` are
    the same picture, so no measurement of the page can choose. The
    raster cannot repair that class at all.
  * MathPix DROPS explicit spacing rather than adding it -- 0.12x the
    author's rate under 25 characters, 0.73x at 100-199. Worst on
    SHORT expressions, which is the opposite of the expectation.
  * Aligned by similarity, not exact match; the aligner recovers
    known answers 1,474 of 1,475 times.

RE-RUN AFTER THEIR DE-TOKENIZER FIX. Every figure here predates it,
and a bug that invented invisible brackets is in exactly this class.
`tools/fuzzyalign.py` reproduces the whole table in one command.

====================================================================
4. REPAIRING LARGE OPEN MATRIX ARRANGEMENT -- SCOPED, NOT STARTED
====================================================================

Scope agreed: a repair step for EQUATIONS WITH LOW CONFIDENCE ONLY.

Nothing in 639-657 touches it. All of that work is on
`evidence-formula.tex`, which is inline maths;
`evidence-equation.tex` exists alongside it and has not been read.
The low-confidence gate is the same `conf` field already in use, so
the population is available the moment the repair is.

====================================================================
NOT CLAIMED
====================================================================

  * TWO DOCUMENTS, both arXiv, both LaTeX-produced. 155 verdicts.
  * THE 20-ERROR ESTIMATE behind the pricing table comes from one
    sample of 60 with one error in it; its interval runs from 0 to 43,
    so "~1.7 wrong marks" is the right order and not a precise figure.
  * The marking policy is tuned on the same 12 errors it is scored
    against. The only out-of-sample evidence is the blind twenty of
    out/655, where the conjunction held at 36% against 41% in-sample.
  * 0.80 for low confidence is chosen, not calibrated.
  * Nothing here measures how often a mark is MISSING that should be
    there -- a third of expressions now carry none by design.
