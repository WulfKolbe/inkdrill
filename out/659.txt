659 - what happened to the formula evidence: no interface, stale crops, and three defects in inkdrill's own tools
==============================================================================================================

The user's report: pdfdrill published the formula evidence with
confidence values and NO marks; low confidence "shows that the cropping
range is wrong"; example 0902.0431_FO0068. And: check that yesterday's
tested changes are integrated and documented.

They were not. Nothing from 630-658 had reached pdfdrill or any
document: the marking policy lived in `tools/formularesidual.py`,
there was no entry point a consumer could call, and CLAUDE.md,
HANDOVER.md and docs/ did not mention the formula tools at all.

====================================================================
1. WHY THERE ARE NO MARKS
====================================================================

pdfdrill has no consumer for them. `reports/evidence.py` opens:
"Lookup, not reading matter. No ink, no legend, no findings, no
cell-rect marks." A grep of pdfdrill's src/ finds no reference to
formulafind, formularesidual or any inkdrill formula output. inkdrill
had nothing for it to call either -- until now: section 4.

====================================================================
2. FO0068, AND THE STALE CROPS BEHIND IT
====================================================================

FO0068's published crop is the RIGHT RECTANGLE CUT FROM THE WRONG
PAGE: the region {491, 873, 699x48} of its host line on page 4, cut
from page 177 instead (correlation with page 177 at that rectangle
+0.714; with the host line -0.018). The size is exactly what the
region predicts, which is why a size check passed it.

The cause is pdfdrill's crop cache. `render_crops` skips any title
whose file exists (`if f.is_file() and f.stat().st_size > 500:
cached += 1; continue`), so a crop is rendered ONCE and never again,
whatever the host-line join does later. FO0068's was rendered
2026-09-03 09:09, ten minutes after pdfdrill's c6c184c3, under the
join as it stood then; the first-occurrence rule pdfdrill uses today
arrived with 42b92d15 (2026-09-06). `report-crops-b` is a downscale
of `report-crops`, so it inherits every stale crop.

HOW MANY, corpus-wide. `tools/cropcheck.py` compares every published
crop with its row's host line cut LOSSLESS from `inspect/pages`,
resampled to the crop's size, by pixel correlation. 21 documents,
37,610 rows, 37,491 checkable:

    document                  checked  wrong
    1510.06699                  1,059     85   8.03%
    0902.0431                   3,163     60   1.90%
    2010.14265                    368      7   1.90%
    2501.06662                    385      3   0.78%
    2106.07890                    318      2   0.63%
    bradley_spring22              181      2   1.10%
    the other 15 (all books)   31,017      0
    TOTAL                      37,491    159   0.42%

Every stale crop is in an arXiv-era document; the 15 books are clean.
Within a document they come in RUNS of consecutive ids (0902.0431
FO0007-FO0077; 1510.06699 FO0069-0083, FO0585-0593, FO0651-0679),
i.e. whole stretches of lines, not scattered rows. Three shapes, all
seen by eye: a different page (FO0068); the line directly ABOVE the
host (1510.06699 FO0719, FO0799, FO0669, FO0904); the same words at a
different occurrence (0902.0431 FO0034 shows the bold centred
"Exceptional Lie group E6", its host is the "III." heading).

THE CUT, 0.35, is placed by eye in an empty interval. The highest row
judged wrong is 0.262 and the lowest judged right 0.413, and no row
of the 37,491 lies between: 5 of 5 wrong below, 10 of 10 right from
0.413 to 0.548. The first value, 0.5, came from 0902.0431 alone and
called four correct crops wrong -- thin glyphs, a sub-pixel shift, and
penev_A is a scan.

THE REMEDY IS PDFDRILL'S, and is not done here: key the crop cache by
(page, region) rather than by title, or invalidate and re-render. The
159 ids are listed at the end of this file.

====================================================================
3. "LOW CONFIDENCE SHOWS THE CROP IS WRONG" -- REFUTED
====================================================================

A formula row's confidence and page are its HOST LINE's
(`FormulaRow.shown_confidence` returns `host_line.confidence`): the
number is MathPix's opinion of the whole line, and says nothing about
where a crop was cut. Measured on the 37,491 checked rows:

    host-line confidence   rows    wrong crop
    >= 0.8               33,676    146   0.43%
    <  0.8                3,815     13   0.34%

No association. A low-confidence row belongs in the residual report
because the producer is unsure of the line, which is what 658 decided;
it is not evidence about the crop.

====================================================================
4. WHAT IS NOW INTEGRATED (abfc7f7)
====================================================================

`tools/formulamarks.py run <bibkey> --work DIR` then `marks`: one JSON
document on stdout, pdfdrill's `reportpages` convention. Per row
`mark`, `why_no_mark`, `rect` in MathPix pixels relative to the host
region (a pixel of pdfdrill's full-size crop), `rect_frac`,
`host_page`, `region`, `flags`. Refuses the document when the rows
(id, maths) or `lines.json` changed since the run, and names why.
The contract is in HANDOVER, "Formula evidence".

Checked by eye on pdfdrill's OWN crops of 1605.05775: 7 of 7 marks on
the right expression, some edges a glyph early or late (the known left
bias). 26 of 138 rows marked there against 66% on 0902.0431 -- its
expressions are short (median 3 gaps, 47 single symbols), and the
closed policy suppresses exactly those.

The corpus run (21 documents) is in progress; its counts are out/660.

WHAT A MARK CANNOT REACH. 67 rows carry a published crop but no line
inkdrill can find: 58 because the evidence shows a REWRITTEN reading
(`\operatorname{\mathbf{Rel}}` where MathPix's line holds
`\boldsymbol{\operatorname{Rel}}`) and pdfdrill keys the host line on
the raw reading, which the evidence file does not carry; 6 not
rendered; 3 with no similar span. They appear under `not_measured`,
never with a guessed line. pdfdrill can close the 58 by passing its
host line or the raw latex.

====================================================================
5. THREE DEFECTS IN INKDRILL'S OWN TOOLS (fixed, pinned)
====================================================================

`tests/test_formulatools.py`, 20 hermetic tests, fixture numbers
measured.

a. `rows()` parsed the whole table with one lazy regex that demanded
   every cell. A row with no published crop took the NEXT row's
   picture and swallowed that row: 0902.0431 lost FO0020, FO0022,
   FO0052, FO0053, FO0065, FO0071 and FO0341 from every measurement
   since 639, and FO0019, FO0021, FO0051, FO0064, FO0070 and FO0340
   carried a neighbour's picture. `\lowconf{...}` leaked into 35 ids of
   1510.06699. Rows are now parsed from `\ident` to `\ident` -- a row
   terminator also occurs inside an `array` in the maths (johnston
   FO2040) -- and a missing cell is None. 37,610 of 37,610 idents, 0
   polluted ids.

   What this touches in 630-658: those measurements cut their crops
   from the lossless page, so the placement figures stand for the rows
   they measured; seven rows were never measured, and the six
   mis-paired rows were grouped onto a neighbour's line for the
   OVERLAP and ORDER rules.

b. The MathPix -> page-image mapping read ONE ratio off page 1's width.
   MathPix's page is the CropBox and `inspect/pages` the MediaBox; on
   the four books whose CropBox is inset (cardona, voloshin, gilmore,
   kohlhase-omdoc) every crop was the wrong strip -- the check first
   reported those two books 100% wrong, and the error was mine.
   `page_frames`/`frame_of` map per page, per axis, inset-aware, and
   refuse what they cannot map (0 pages of 21 documents refused).
   Correlation with the published crops, 1-in-40 sample: gilmore
   +0.959, cardona +0.924, voloshin +0.946, omdoc +0.854. None of
   630-658 measured an inset document, so no earlier figure moves.

c. Host lines by a per-page cursor, not pdfdrill's rule. Now a copy of
   `inlinectx` (first span in document order, by equality); it and
   the old cursor agree on 37,503 of 37,503 rows both place. A copy
   can drift, so the consumer compares `region`.

Full suite 1,247 tests, 37 skipped.

====================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
====================================================================

  * The content check detects a crop showing a DIFFERENT LINE. A crop
    of the right line cut a few pixels off would correlate high and
    pass; that is not measured here.
  * The cut is eye-placed on 15 rows either side of an empty interval,
    not calibrated on labelled data.
  * 119 rows could not be checked: 52 have no published crop, 67 no
    host line inkdrill can find (section 4).
  * The stale-crop ORIGIN (the 09-03 join) is inferred from one row's
    timestamps and pdfdrill's commit history, not re-run.
