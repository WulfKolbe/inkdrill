285 - matching tex.zip images to lines.json regions: no population
========================================================================

ANSWER: the matcher is NOT built, because there are no old-format
documents on this corpus to run it on. Measured, not assumed, and
measured independently of the session that raised the same doubt.

    matched      20,565 of 20,582   by DICTIONARY LOOKUP, not matching
    ambiguous         0
    unmatched        17             and none of them has a region

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CENSUS

Every `*.tex.zip` in the library, every image entry inside it:

    zips                          1,216
    image entries                20,582
      coordinate form            20,565    -<page>_<h>_<w>_<y>_<x>
      collision form                  0    -<page>_<n>(<m>)
      neither                        17
    unreadable zips                   0

The collision form is what 285 exists for -- a filename that names a
figure by an index rather than by its geometry, so the region has to
be recovered by comparison. There are none. I checked for `(`, `)`,
`[`, `]`, `%28` and `%29` anywhere in the entry name or its path.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE COORDINATE FORM IS THE REGION -- VERIFIED, NOT INHERITED

If the filename already carries page, height, width, top_left_y and
top_left_x, then no comparison is needed: it is a key. That claim
came from another session and I did not take it on trust.

Over 60 documents sampled at seed 5, every coordinate-form image
looked up against its own document's `lines.json`:

    coordinate images        645
    EXACT region match       645
    match rate           100.00%

Not "close", not "within a tolerance" -- the four numbers in the
filename equal the four numbers in the region. Aspect ratio and the
five-tuple have nothing to add to an exact key.

`image-not-found.png` is a PLACEHOLDER -- the extraction failed and
this stands in its place. It corresponds to no region because there
is no figure behind it; matching it to the nearest region by aspect
ratio would attach a real rectangle to a failure.

`smile-<sha1>.png` is a chemical structure rendered from SMILES, not
cropped from the page. Its name is a hash of the molecule. It
corresponds to no page region either, and its aspect ratio is a
property of the renderer.

Both classes are UNMATCHED and should stay unmatched. That is the
answer for all 17.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHY NOT BUILD IT ANYWAY

The task's own warning is the reason. It says an ambiguous match must
not be resolved by taking the first candidate, because 282 did that
and produced confidently wrong filenames.

A matcher built here would be tested on zero positives. Its ambiguity
handling -- the part that went wrong before -- would have no case to
exercise it, and the first real input would be its first test. Every
threshold in it (how close two aspect ratios must be, how close two
five-tuples) would be chosen from nothing.

The strongest form of not repeating 282's fallback defect is not
writing the fallback. A matcher with no population is a fallback path
waiting for its first caller.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS: one document carrying the collision form. If
one exists outside this library, the census here says nothing about
it -- 1,216 zips is what we hold, not what MathPix can emit. Send one
and the task becomes answerable, with a real positive to test the
ambiguous branch against.
