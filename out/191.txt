191 - the pooled distribution, with and without a dominant document
========================================================================

RULE: any document supplying more than 15% of a population's rows is
reported separately, and every figure previously quoted over that
population is restated with and without it. The threshold is the
task's; it is applied per POPULATION, because the same corpus run
produces three different populations and a different document
dominates each.

0911.3722 supplies 253 of 426 rows -- 59.4% -- and this is the case
the rule exists for.

    figure                          as quoted   without 0911.3722
    negative-delta rows                   426                 173
    rows with a rule in the render        110                 110
    rows with NO rule                     316                  63

117 SAID: "74% of the population has no rule in the rendered cell.
Those rows are counted and named, not dropped: a measurement about
rules over a population that is three-quarters ruleless would be
quoting a subgroup as a corpus."

The sentence was right and the number was one document. 0911.3722
contributes 253 ruleless rows and ZERO rule-bearing ones. Take it out
and the ruleless share falls from 74.2% to 36.4%.

THE ANSWER ITSELF IS UNTOUCHED, and for a reason worth stating: all
110 rule-bearing rows come from the other 33 documents, so removing
the dominant document removes nothing from the measured subset.

    pooled deficit near a rule    73/425 = 17.2%   unchanged

Identical to the digit. 117's conclusion -- that the deficit is NOT
near the rules, and that the pooled figure said otherwise -- stands
without qualification. What was distorted was the DENOMINATOR I used
to describe the population, not the finding.

5. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY

- NOT that 15% is a principled threshold. It is the task's, and it
  happens to separate cleanly here: the next-largest contributors are
  11.8% (102) and 6.6% (117), so no document sits near the line.
- NOT that a document under 15% cannot distort a figure. 117's
  measured subset is 110 rows over 33 documents; a document supplying
  20 of those would move it and would not be caught by this rule.
- NOT a re-measurement. Every number here is arithmetic over
  `results/overrun-20260822-141104.tsv`,
  `results/ruledeficit-20260822-201421.tsv` and
  `results/compare-roster-scan-20260823-014120.tsv`, which is why it
  was cheap and why it should have been done when those files were
  written.
