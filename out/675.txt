675 - the fit score against 45 eye verdicts: every wrong mark is below 0.60
==========================================================================

pdfdrill declined to ask for a score clause tuned to one row (FO2204,
out/674 §3a) and said the measurement was worth having either way. This
is it.

METHOD. `tools/marksample.py`: 45 marked rows from the 21 placed mark
sets, banded by `formulafind`'s fit score, seed 674, at most two per
document per band, each rectangle drawn on the LOSSLESS cut of its host
line at 400 dpi. The label showed the id and the reading, never the
score. Verdicts: RIGHT covers the reading; OFF covers it but over- or
under-runs by a token; WRONG covers something else. Verdicts, scores and
readings: ~/inkdrill-marks/score-verdicts.json.

====================================================================
1. THE RESULT
====================================================================

    band        marks      sample verdicts
    < 0.50         39      3 right                   2 wrong
    0.50-0.60      93                     4 off      4 wrong
    0.60-0.70     354      5 right        3 off

    TOTAL       8,383     32 right        7 off      6 wrong

EVERY WRONG MARK IS BELOW 0.60. Every off-by-a-token is below 0.70. All
24 sampled at 0.70 or above are right.

The six wrong ones, with what the rectangle actually covers:

    0.446  johnston FO2227   takes the "(b)" label, drops the last column
    0.471  fong-spivak FO2204  a figure region; the reading is not in it
    0.587  lyche FO3734      the words "nverges for a 2 by 2 matrix A := ["
    0.590  lyche FO0540      the words "umn k from column k+1 f"
    0.595  fong-spivak FO3331  starts at "that", ends mid-expression
    0.598  voloshin FO1639   a span far wider than the reading

WHY THE EXISTING CLAUSES CANNOT CATCH THEM. In all six, MARGIN EQUALS
THE SCORE to four decimals: the template matches nothing else on the
line either, so the position is "unique" and out/658's margin clause
passes it. Margin says how much better the best position is than the
next; score says whether the best position is a match at all. They are
different channels and only the second sees these rows.

    cut     suppresses                sample below the cut
    0.50       39 marks (0.5%)        3 right, 0 off, 2 wrong
    0.60      132 marks (1.6%)        3 right, 4 off, 6 wrong
    0.65      257 marks (3.1%)        5 right, 4 off, 6 wrong
    0.70      486 marks (5.8%)        8 right, 7 off, 6 wrong
    0.80    1,850 marks (22.1%)      16 right, 7 off, 6 wrong

0.60 removes every wrong mark in the sample at a cost of 3 right per 13
suppressed -- about 30 correct marks of 8,383 by that rate, against
roughly 45-60 wrong ones. 0.70 costs nearly twice as many right marks
and catches no additional wrong one.

WHAT THE RIGHT ONES BELOW 0.60 ARE: all three are small DISPLAY MATRICES
(`[1 2 / 0 1]`, `(1 2 3 / 4 5 6)`) hosted inline. Their template is a
bracketed array whose rendering differs from the print, so the score is
low while the position is right -- which is why a cut costs anything at
all.

====================================================================
2. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
====================================================================

  * 45 verdicts, mine, not blind to the BAND (the sample is stratified,
    so each montage is one band) though blind to the score itself. The
    low bands are 5 and 8 rows.
  * 0 wrong of 32 sampled at 0.60 or above bounds that rate only
    loosely: the 95% upper bound is about 9%, i.e. it does not prove the
    upper 8,251 marks are clean.
  * Nothing is changed. out/658's policy stands until a threshold is
    decided; this is the measurement it would rest on, and the decision
    is the user's and pdfdrill's.
  * The score is `formulafind`'s own profile-correlation fit, not a
    property of the page: a low score can mean a bad position OR a
    template that does not look like the print (the matrices above).
