STEP 5 -- measurement
=====================

No code change in this step.

Case distribution -- the premise, now measured on REAL pages
-------------------------------------------------------------
The audit measured 90.9% "pure continue" on a synthetic page of ring
glyphs and flagged it as unverified on corpus ink. The two real page
fixtures in the repo:

                              V       birth   merge   single-parent  pure CONTINUE
arxiv_1408_0838_p8.png   120,494    5.1%    3.2%    91.7%          86.9%
heim_bh2_p96.png          35,283    5.4%    3.0%    91.6%          86.6%
synthetic ring page      176,000    2.3%    2.3%    95.5%          90.9%

"pure continue" = exactly one parent AND that parent has exactly one
child. The premise holds on real ink, 4 points lower than on the
synthetic. Scanned German book and born-digital arXiv agree to within
0.3 points, which is more agreement than I expected.

What this did and did not remove
---------------------------------
Removed, per run, at Capture.NONE:
  - 4.9 of 6.9 union-find finds
  - 2 dict inserts (the (0,0) pair created and immediately popped)
  - the `adj` list and its appends
  - the `roots_before` set + sorted + tuple
  - the `kids_of` setdefault + append per edge
  - the per-line `touched` set (one find per run)
  - a root comparison on the first parent of every run
Unions are unchanged at exactly the previous count, asserted by
test_union_count_unchanged: they are the real work.

NOT removed, and now the remaining per-run cost at Capture.GRAPH:
  - the RunNode dataclass allocation and the page-wide `nodes` list
  - the up/down list appends
  - the Event objects
None of these is touchable without the closure stream, which is out of
scope for this CR. That is why GRAPH barely moves: its cost was never
in the union-find.
