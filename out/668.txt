THE SIX WAYS IT FAILS, each seen on a drawn grid:

  F1 THE REGION IS THE DISPLAY LINE, NOT THE ARRAY. `A :=` before a
     matrix and `\in R^{n x n}` after it sit on ONE of its rows, so every
     other row reads empty there (lyche EQ0834, EQ0010; 0902.0431 EQ1178).
  F2 LINES FRAGMENT. `c_{1,1}` has three components and becomes a line
     of its own; a diagonal-dot row splits; tall sums and brackets shred
     a display into 23 lines (johnston EQ1040, EQ0005; gilmore EQ0583;
     lyche EQ0010).
  F3 ONE CONTINUOUS WIDTH CLASS. With no 1.5x jump every gutter is a
     column, so a `\cdots` and a `\otimes 1^0` are cut apart
     (cardona EQ0441). The rule came from johnston EQ0486, where all
     gutters really were columns.
  F4 MARGINS READ AS EMPTY. Two alignments that share a column pitch
     pass the registration test and merge; the shorter one's margin
     becomes 48 empty cells (0902.0431 EQ1033). The EQ0756 shape again,
     now on the wrong side of the test.
  F5 THE HOST LINE IS ONE ROW OF THE DISPLAY. lines.json splits a tall
     matrix into several lines; the cut holds one of them
     (johnston EQ0735).
  F6 AN UNALIGNED ROW KILLS THE COLUMNS. A gutter must be blank in every
     row of a block, so `minimize: 3y1 + 3y2 + ...` set without alignment
     erases the columns of the constraints under it, and the blanks
     inside them are never seen (johnston EQ0543, EQ0524, EQ0562).

====================================================================
4. THE GATE, AND WHY IT IS NOT WHAT THE OVERLAY NEEDS
====================================================================

`agree` -- ink cells per row equal the reading's non-empty cells per
row -- selected 8 grids and all 8 are right. It is the only automatic
selection here that held.

It selects exactly the rows where MathPix ALREADY read the cells right.
The overlay exists for the others: johnston EQ0486 (8 columns read for
11) and 0902.0431 EQ0756 (13 for 14) both fail the gate by construction.
So `agree` validates the GRID; it cannot choose overlay TARGETS. Choosing
targets needs F1, F2 and F6 fixed, so that a disagreement means MathPix
and not the grid.

`~/inkdrill-marks/whitespace/grids.json` now holds the 12 grids verified
by eye (the 10 above plus EQ0756 and johnston EQ0486), each with how it
was verified -- 51 empty cells in all. Nothing else from this run is
handed over.
