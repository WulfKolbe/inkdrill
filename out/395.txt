FIVE CLASSES, WHICH IS THE CHECK THAT MATTERS. A single-class distribution
would be a pairing failure wearing a result's clothes: if the rows were
attached to the wrong equations, or all to the same one, the flags would
collapse. Five classes spread over 411 rows, with a median distance of 3 and
a tail to 357, is a measurement.

The 27 `component` rows are the candidates for a new MathPix finding; 396
lists them.
