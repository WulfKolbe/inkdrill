325 / 329 - five candidate discriminators, none of which separate
========================================================================

ANSWER: none of the five separates the signal population from
ordinary low-confidence equations. Two point the wrong way, one is
degenerate, and the ink-versus-LaTeX ratio -- the one that was meant
to be immune to the others' defect -- fails for a reason visible in
the named example's own LaTeX.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE POPULATION IS MINE, NOT 324's

324 is pdfdrill's task and out/324.txt was not written when this ran,
so these populations are MY construction from 324's stated criteria:

    low-confidence (<0.3) math lines           1,807
      SIGNAL   array + >=2 arrows, or array
               + \downarrow/\uparrow             157   58 documents
      ORDINARY                                 1,650
      control sampled to match                   157
    measured                                     40 + 40

pdfdrill's own count is 49 against my 157. That is a difference in
CONSTRUCTION, not in the corpus, and nothing here travels between
sessions until it has been re-run on ONE list. It will be re-run on
theirs rather than reconciled -- merging two constructions makes a
third set neither session measured.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE FOUR STRUCTURAL CANDIDATES

    statistic            signal p50   control p50   control >= signal p50
    components                93.00         88.00          50%
    components per row        13.00          9.28          38%
    distinct rows              7.00          8.00          65%
    x-centre columns          18.00         21.00          68%
    thin off-baseline          0.00          0.00         100%

COMPONENTS PER ROW is the only one with any direction, and at 38% it
still puts more than a third of ordinary equations above the signal
median, with the ranges overlapping at both ends (p10 3.34 against
3.86, p90 25.8 against 23.6).

DISTINCT ROWS AND COLUMN CLUSTERS POINT THE WRONG WAY. Both are LOWER
in the signal. An invented `aligned` block produces FEWER rows than a
real multi-line equation, not more, so a detector keyed on either
would fire preferentially on the correct transcriptions.

THIN OFF-BASELINE IS DEGENERATE, not weak. Both medians are 0, so
"100% at or above" is the statement 0 >= 0 and carries nothing.
`is_rule`-shaped components off a text baseline essentially do not
occur in either population, which is consistent with 116 and 196:
the aspect gate finds almost nothing on real pages.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHY ALL FOUR FAIL, WHICH IS ONE REASON

Each describes the SHAPE THE READER PRODUCED. A reader that invents
an array produces array-shaped output whether or not the page held
one, so measuring the output's shape cannot reveal that the output
was invented. The four statistics are downstream of the thing they
are trying to detect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE INK-VERSUS-LATEX RATIO (329)

Components in the scan against symbol tokens in the LaTeX. This does
not ask what shape the output has; it asks how much of the page the
output ACCOUNTS FOR, which is the general class and does not care
whether the content was a commutative diagram, a vertex diagram or a
matrix.

    ink components per LaTeX symbol token
       signal   p10 0.87   p50 1.05   p90 1.48
       control  p10 0.84   p50 1.16   p90 1.53
       control at or above the signal median: 68%

IT DOES NOT SEPARATE, and the signal median is LOWER than the
control's.

Tokens are counted as symbols a reader would have had to see:
control sequences, letters, digits, operators. Structural
scaffolding -- \begin{aligned}, &, \\, braces, sizing -- is NOT
counted, because it describes layout rather than ink and counting it
would let an invented array inflate its own denominator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHY IT FAILS, AND IT IS IN EQ0717's OWN LATEX

Cardona EQ0717, source page 275, is transcribed as:

    \begin{aligned} & \hat{T}=\operatorname{tr}_{a} M_{a}(u) \\
    &=\left.\right|_{L}^{L} \cdots \\ & \cdots \cdots \\
    & \cdots 2 \\ & L \end{aligned} a .

The task estimated "perhaps eight meaningful tokens". This counter
reads about twenty, because `\cdots` counts as a symbol -- and there
are five of them.

A READER THAT INVENTS AN ARRAY ALSO INVENTS FILLER. The denominator
inflates alongside the numerator and the ratio stays near 1. The
invention pays for itself, which is the same defect as the four
structural candidates arriving by a different route: the token count
is also downstream of the invention.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE POST-HOC VARIANT, AND IT DOES NOT RESCUE IT

An ellipsis denotes CONTENT OMITTED, so a token meaning "something is
missing here" arguably cannot account for ink that is present.
Excluding `\cdots`, `\ldots`, `\vdots`, `\ddots` and `\dots`:

       signal   p10 0.87   p50 1.05   p90 1.48
       control  p10 0.84   p50 1.16   p90 1.54
       control at or above the signal median: 68%

Unchanged to two decimal places on every figure that matters. On
EQ0717's document the exclusion moves one example from 1.88 to 1.94
and nothing else.

Two things follow. The variant fails, so no argument is needed about
whether it was legitimate -- and it WAS decided after seeing the
primary result, which is the move that turns a measurement into a
fit, so it is reported here as post-hoc and would need a population
chosen before the exclusion was known.

The ellipsis hypothesis is also refuted as a mechanism: filler
inflates the count, but not by enough to matter across the
population. EQ0717 is not typical of the 157.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS SUGGESTS, WITHOUT BUILDING ANYTHING

Every candidate measured so far is computed from the reader's OWN
OUTPUT, or from ink normalised by it. An invention supplies both
sides, so none of them can see it. A discriminator would have to come
from something the reader does not control:

  * the region's ink against the ink of its NEIGHBOURS on the page --
    a diagram transcribed as an equation is still diagram-shaped
    relative to the body text around it
  * the rendered LaTeX against the scan, which is what the compare
    channel already measures and what out/102 and out/117 are about
  * pdfdrill's own confidence, which is already the selector and
    cannot also be the test

The second is the one that already exists. A row in this population
should show a large component delta in `compare`, and that is
checkable against the existing per-document TSVs without building
anything new. It is the obvious next measurement and it is not this
task.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. POPULATION

40 signal and 40 control regions, cropped from each document's OWN
source pdf at 300 dpi via the lines.json region, from my construction
of 324's criteria over 1,216 documents. Regions under 6 components or
20 px in either axis excluded. Confidence read from lines.json's
`confidence` field.

NOT a corpus rate: 80 regions, one construction, and the selector
(confidence < 0.3) is itself a MathPix judgement rather than ground
truth.
