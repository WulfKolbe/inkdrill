386 — a defect located in reportcompare, not fixed here
=======================================================

Found while converting the DTZ report's measurement into residuals. One page
of 34 lost a row, and the cause is an index used against the wrong population
— the class this codebase already names in several places.

  tools/reportcompare.py:390   png  = d / f"p{p:03d}_r300.png"
                               hts  = {r: height of 300-dpi lattice row r}
                        :400   if ln == 0 or hts.get(ln, 999) < 40: continue

`ln` is the row index from the md file, which `inkdrill compare` produced from
ITS lattice. `hts` is indexed by a lattice re-derived here from the 300 dpi
render. The two agree only when both resolutions detect the same number of
rows. Measured:

  p021 @300  8 rows x 6 cols      p021 @600  8 rows x 6 cols    agree
  p022 @300  8 rows x 6 cols      p022 @600  5 rows x 6 cols    DISAGREE

Not a tolerance effect. p022 gives 5 rows at 600 dpi at tol 2, 4, 6 and 8, and
8 rows at 300 dpi at tol 2, 4, 6 and 12.

Matching the row bands by POSITION (300 dpi doubled into 600 dpi coordinates)
shows what the extra rows are:

  row 0   300x2=(430,528)     600=(429,528)      match      header
  row 1   300x2=(532,2228)    600=(532,2229)     match      data
  row 2   300x2=(1922,1928)   600=(2233,3830)    differ     <- 6 px rule
  row 3   300x2=(2234,3830)   600=(3835,5432)    differ
  row 4   300x2=(3624,3630)   600=(5436,5634)    differ     <- 6 px rule
  row 5   300x2=(3836,5432)   600=None
  row 6   300x2=(5226,5232)   600=None                      <- 6 px rule
  row 7   300x2=(5436,5634)   600=None

The 600 dpi lattice finds the 5 REAL rows — header, three data rows, footer.
The 300 dpi lattice finds those five plus three spurious thin rules INSIDE
figures, 6 px tall. The sliver filter exists to remove exactly those, and on
33 of 34 pages it does. On p022 it removed a real 1597 px data row instead,
because md row 2 looked up 300-dpi row 2, one of the 6 px rules.

SECOND, AND WHY THE PAGE CANNOT BE REPAIRED FROM THE md:

  inkdrill/__main__.py:707
    if nrows["A"] != nrows["B"]:
        print("row counts differ ... comparing the first {min}")

compare pairs the two lattices BY INDEX when the counts differ. On p022 that
compares A's 6 px rule against B's second data row. The L column of that page
is taken from the wrong rows, so the page has to be re-measured; no
re-aggregation of the existing md can recover it.

THE FIX, and why it is not applied here

Slivers must be dropped from each lattice INDEPENDENTLY, before the two are
paired, so a row index means the same thing on both sides. reportcompare must
then take heights from the lattice that produced the md rather than
re-deriving one.

That changes row indices in every md file the corpus holds, so it belongs with
a re-measurement rather than as a patch under a running job. It was located
while a measurement was in flight and is recorded rather than applied.

CONSEQUENCE, already contained on the pdfdrill side

inkconvert now pairs per page, so this costs 3 rows (DTZ00062, DTZ00063,
DTZ00064) instead of refusing all 100. The DTZ report carries residuals on 97
of 100 rows; those three read inkUnmeasured until p022 is re-measured.
