horizontal line running its whole width, meeting once. The `|` of
`{lll|l}` and the `\hline` intersect, and 8-connectivity fuses them

POSITION RELATIVE TO THE CELL GRID:

    vertical    centre x-offset 412 of 548  =  0.752 of the width

For a 4-column, 4-row grid the boundaries after column 3 and row 3
lie at 0.750. Measured 0.752 and 0.743. The rules are where
`{lll|l}` and a fourth-row `\hline` put them, to within a pixel and
a half.

is what a bracket is. The cross has one horizontal band, in the
middle. That difference is measurable and is what separates a rule
crossing from a delimiter; nothing in the current code uses it.

All 59 fall inside the region MathPix labelled `math`. The LaTeX
names nine label WORDS -- up, down, strange, charm, flavors, red,
yellow, blue, lilac -- plus two arrows, and every one of them is
inside the equation's bbox. None is outside it.

A COUNT OF WORDS IS NOT AVAILABLE FROM THIS SIDE, and saying so is
part of the answer. `mathstruct.group()` returns 58 clusters from
those 59 components, because G6 joins only STACKED components -- an
`i` with its dot -- and never horizontally adjacent letters. It is a
glyph former, not a word former, and this project has no word former
because it reads no text. The defensible units are 59 components and
7 rows; "nine labels" is the LaTeX's count, not the ink's.

THE REGION BOUNDARY IS CLEAN, which is worth recording because 102
found the opposite case elsewhere: the 80 scanlines immediately below
the region carry ZERO ink across the full page width. The region ends
where the content ends. These labels are inside the equation's bbox
because MathPix put them there, not because the crop overran.

4. WHAT THIS ADDS TO 196

196 found the merged cross on three 2x2 arrays where the rule crossed
at the centre. This is the same defect at 0.75 rather than 0.50, on a
4x4, which rules out any reading that depends on the rule being
central. One component, two rules, and `ink.rules[]` reports neither.

It also adds the bracket contrast, which 196 did not have cleanly: a
bracket and a rule crossing are both tall low-fill components, and
they differ in the NUMBER and POSITION of their full-span horizontal
bands -- two at the ends against one in the middle. Any future split
must separate them, and the profile computed here already does.
