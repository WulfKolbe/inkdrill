====================================================================
2. THE EYE CHECK, AND A UNIT MISTAKE IT NEARLY HID
====================================================================

16 marks corpus-wide gained a new or moved rectangle. All 16 drawn on
the lossless cut of their host line:

    13 right, 1 off by a token, 2 WRONG

    off    penev_A FO0008   leaves the reading's leading `1` outside
    wrong  gilmore FO2420   the reading is a single `\alpha`; the box
                            spans `alpha -> Z alpha`
    wrong  fong-7s FO2524   covers `p = (x` of `p=(x,y) \in U`

THE FIRST DRAWING WAS WRONG AND THE VERDICTS WITH IT. `marksample.draw`
takes a MARKS row, whose `rect` is in MathPix page pixels, and multiplies
by the frame scale. I handed it `formulafind`'s own record, whose `rect`
is already in CROP pixels -- so every box came out 1.6x too large and
shifted, and 12 of 14 looked wrong. Nothing was placed on that reading;
redrawn from the marks rows, the same 16 are 13 right. The tell was the
shape of the error: every box wrong the SAME way (too wide, shifted
right) is a units bug, not a measurement.

A COUNTEREXAMPLE WORTH KEEPING. gilmore FO2420 scores 0.9338. out/675
found 24 of 24 sampled at 0.70+ right, and this is a wrong mark far above
that -- so the 0.60 cut is a floor on badness, not a guarantee of
goodness, exactly as out/675's "does not prove the upper 8,251 marks are
clean" said. The two wrong marks are reported to pdfdrill by id; they can
be suppressed individually if the user wants them out before publishing,
which is not the same as moving a threshold.

that is not the class being removed.
