1. WHAT CHANGED IN THE TOOLS
====================================================================

  formulafind   `first_occurrence_lines` now skips a line whose type
                cannot host a transclusion and reads `text_display or
                text`, as pdfdrill's `inlinectx` does. The 17 types are
                copied as `NON_PROSE_HOSTS`, in pdfdrill's five named
                families; a test compares the set with their
                `docmodel.line_types` when `INKDRILL_PDFDRILL_SRC` names
                their src (skipped otherwise), so a change on their side
                fails here rather than drifting again.
  formulamarks  `update` gained its second trigger: a measured row whose
                host line MOVED -- or has none now -- is measured again at
                the stored scale, whatever its reading says. Without it a
                re-hosting cannot be applied at all: the reading is
                identical, so nothing else notices.
  formulafind   `render` no longer dies when ghostscript refuses a pdf
                pdflatex wrote. It returns False and the row reports
                "render failed", as a missing pdf already did. One
                kohlhase-omdoc row ended a whole update this way.

====================================================================
3. TWO THINGS THE RE-HOSTING EXPOSED, NEITHER FIXED
====================================================================

a. THE MARKING POLICY NEVER LOOKS AT THE FIT SCORE. FO2204's reading
   `\eta: I \rightarrow X` is placed on a `diagram` line -- a 143x36
   figure region -- with score 0.4713, 6 template blobs against 1 in the
   rectangle, and it is MARKED: out/658's four clauses test line
   likeness, gaps, margin and edge cuts, and nothing else. Its old host
   suppressed it by the edge-cut clause, which is why it surfaces now.
   It is not a regression: 1,850 of the 8,383 marks (22%) have a score
   below 0.80, and 1,851 of 8,413 did before. `formularesidual` flags
   them POOR_FIT; pdfdrill draws them anyway. Changing the policy needs
   fresh eye verdicts (out/658), so this is recorded, not acted on.

b. A RUNNING HEAD CAN BE A HOST. pdfdrill's set excludes tables,
   headings, titles, TOC entries and figure labels -- not `page_info`.
   gilmore FO0001 (`U(2) \rightarrow H_{4}`) now hosts on the running
   head of page 225 instead of a body line on page 10, and its mark is
   correct THERE. 8 rows corpus-wide host on a `page_info` line, 4 of
   them marked; also 37 on `footnote`, 11 on `qed_symbol`, 11 on
   `equation_number`, 8 on `code`, 236 on `diagram`. Their own
   transclusion rule excludes repeating frames, so `page_info` looks
   like an omission in their set -- reported, theirs to decide.
