CORRECTION FIRST. out/646 closed by proposing that
`0902.0431.tex.zip` would settle whether the page carries `\mid`.
IT WOULD NOT HAVE. That archive holds
`1deb350a-8d75-4da5-b1ec-153e0bfd7145.tex` and an images folder --
IT IS MATHPIX'S OWN OUTPUT. Checking MathPix against MathPix would
have confirmed the substitution and proved nothing, and the CR this
work started from says the same thing about OpenCV in one line: an
oracle that shares a dependency with the thing it checks is not an
oracle. I proposed a contaminated one and it was caught for me.

THE REAL SOURCE WAS IN THE SAME DIRECTORY. `0902.0431.tgz` is not a
tarball: it is a gzip of a single file, `SpEcxp.tex`, last modified
2009-02-03. That is the arXiv e-print -- the AUTHOR'S OWN LATEX,
607,943 bytes, written before MathPix existed as an input to anything
here.

wider bar on every set-builder in the document, and the hypothesis
out/645 raised as a lead is confirmed from outside the instrument.
