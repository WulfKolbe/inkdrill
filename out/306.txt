306 - advisory space positions: not emitted
========================================================================

306 IS NOT BUILT, because 305 measured the signal it would emit and
the signal does not separate.

305's result, on `math` and `text` regions of one book, 202 rows and
7,894 gaps: the ratio of a gap to its row's median gap runs from
87.6% of rows above 2x to 36.1% above 10x, smoothly, with no knee.
There is no threshold at which "this gap is a typeset space" is a
different statement from "this gap is between two glyphs".

WHAT EMITTING IT ANYWAY WOULD COST, and this is why the answer is a
refusal rather than a lower-confidence emission. 306's output is
advisory data for an LLM placing `\quad` and `\;`. The consumer
cannot check it: a model handed "an explicit space is required at
character 14" has no way to tell a measured space from an artefact
of a median that collapsed to 1 px. It would place the command,
the LaTeX would compile, and the result would look correct.

That is worse than 220, which was refused for building before
measuring. Here the measurement exists and says the signal is not
there. Emitting it would be publishing a number the instrument does
not support, into a consumer that cannot audit it.

WHAT WOULD MAKE 306 BUILDABLE. Not a threshold on this statistic --
a different NORMALISER. 305 section 4 records why this one fails: the
row median gap is 1.00 px on half the rows, because adjacent glyphs
in set mathematics touch, so dividing by it saturates the ratio. A
denominator that cannot collapse would be the thing to measure:

  * the row's x-height, or its median glyph WIDTH
  * the MODE of the positive gaps rather than their median
  * the inter-word gap of the surrounding body text, which is a
    property of the typeface rather than of the row

Any of those is a measurement, not a build, and the same shape as
305: take one book, plot the distribution, look for a break, and
report that there is none if there is none.

I have not measured any of them and I am not asserting that one would
work.

See out/305.txt for the measurement.
