out/641's NULL TEST WAS SOUND AND ANSWERED A NARROWER QUESTION

641 applied LANCZOS 62.5% and JPEG q92 to a CORRECTLY CUT crop and
measured the five-tuple: components moved by -1 on 2 of 20, holes on 4
of 20. Scattered, about one crop in ten. Everything in it stands.

But `report-crops-b` differs from the source line by more than a
resample. FO0059's report crop is 635 x 24 and BLANK where the line
carries sixteen components; FO0045's is 159 x 22 and shows different
text entirely. No downsample does that. The real confound is CROP
SELECTION, and 641 could not see it because it never compared a report
crop against its own source line -- it resampled a crop we cut
ourselves and compared that against itself.

The lesson is the one this project keeps re-learning in new clothes: a
null test bounds the effect it models, and says nothing about the
effect it did not. I measured the resample because the resample was
the thing I had thought of.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STANDING RULE FROM THIS

  MEASURE ON THE LOSSLESS SOURCE. A residuals report is a handful of
  rows and can afford any resolution it likes; there is no argument
  for measuring it on a publishing artefact. If the published page
  needs a small JPEG, that is a SECOND pass over the same rows, after
  the measurement, and it never feeds back into it.
