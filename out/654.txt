654 - the hold-out: the rule is a screen, the conjunction is the detector
=========================================================================

60 rows drawn at random from the 481 that `edge cuts >= 1 and gaps <= 7`
flags and the per-length floor does not. Reviewed by eye.

    ONE WRONG IN SIXTY.

    FO2157  matched half the WORD BEFORE the target; the true target
            is further left.

    precision 1.7%, exact 95% CI [0.0%, 8.9%]

Same failure mode as FO0512 in the previous set -- a shorter piece of
text just before the expression taken instead of it. That class has
now appeared twice in two independent samples.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE SAMPLE WAS RANDOM AND NOT RANKED

The 481 could have been sorted by edge cuts and the top reviewed. That
would have measured the RANKING. The question was the rule, so the
draw is seeded and unbiased, and the answer is a rate rather than a
list.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NEITHER SIGNAL WORKS ALONE. THE CONJUNCTION DOES

    region                             rows   errors   precision
    per-length floor AND the rule        17        7       41.2%
    floor only (no cuts, or >7 gaps)     58        0        0.0%
    rule only (not floor-flagged)       481       ~8         1.7%

    the floor alone                      75        7        9.3%
    the rule alone                      498      ~15        3.0%

    placed rows                        2225
    estimated placement errors          ~15        0.7%

THE BASE RATE IS 0.7% AND THE CONJUNCTION IS 41%. That is a lift of
about 24x, and it is the first thing in this chain that would make a
residual report worth opening: SEVENTEEN ROWS out of 3,163.

Each signal on its own is useless. The floor flags 75 to find 7; the
rule flags 498 to find 15. Together they flag 17 to find 7. They are
measuring different things -- "fits worse than its length predicts"
and "the boundary lands inside a glyph" -- and a wrong placement
usually does both.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
AND THE 41.2% IS NOT VALIDATED. THIS MATTERS

The seven errors in the conjunction region are THE SAME SEVEN the rule
was chosen to capture. `edge_cuts >= 1 and gaps <= 7` was picked in
out/653 because it caught all of them, so 41.2% is an IN-SAMPLE figure
and is optimistic by an unknown amount.

The only OUT-OF-SAMPLE number here is the 1.7%, and it is out of
sample precisely because those 481 rows had nothing to do with
choosing the rule.

The conjunction region holds 17 rows and all 17 are now labelled, so
it CANNOT be validated further on this document. It needs a second
document with an author source and an evidence table -- there are six
others -- and that is the next measurement, not another sample here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE NUMBER THE INSTRUMENT ACTUALLY EARNED

    ~15 wrong placements among 2,225 placed rows.
    99.3% of placements are correct.

That is the honest headline, and it took two labelled samples and 135
eye verdicts to get. It also means every red count reported before
this file -- 318, 355, 222 -- was an order of magnitude too large,
because none of them was measured against a label.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDATION, AND IT IS NOT MINE TO TAKE

Report the CONJUNCTION: per-length floor AND edge cuts >= 1 AND
gaps <= 7. Seventeen rows, seven of them real, on this document.

  RECALL IS ABOUT HALF. Roughly 8 of the estimated 15 errors sit in
  the rule-only region and the conjunction misses every one. A
  residual report on this rule is a high-precision, half-blind one,
  and that trade should be stated in the report itself rather than
  discovered by a reader.

Not adopted. `formularesidual` still runs the single floor and
`edge_cut_max` at p90.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT CLAIMED

  * ONE DOCUMENT. 135 labels: 75 from out/653, 60 here.
  * The ~8 errors in the rule-only region are an extrapolation from
    one observation. The CI runs from 0 to 43.
  * NOTHING SAYS WHAT BOTH SIGNALS MISS TOGETHER. The region where
    neither fires -- most of the 2,225 -- has never been sampled, so
    the 0.7% error rate is a floor and not an estimate.
  * The conjunction's precision is in-sample. See above.
