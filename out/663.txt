2. THE 803 WAS TRUE OF THE OLD BUILD. IT IS 88 NOW
====================================================================

On 09-12 the rule "the row's HOST LINE is labelled type=table" counted
803 rows corpus-wide, 460 of them gilmore's. Today the same rule over
the same code gives 88, gilmore 25. Neither number is a mistake: the
rows are gone.

    gilmore rows carrying rendered maths   3,473  ->  3,011   (-462)

-462 against -460: the rows that lost their maths in the rebuild ARE
the table-hosted ones. pdfdrill's transclusion fix has already removed
them, which is why the count collapsed.

Checked before concluding, by running three matching rules side by side
on today's file: first occurrence by span equality gives 25, the row's
own page column by substring gives 4, first line anywhere by substring
gives 25. None reproduces 460, so the difference is not in the rule.

WHAT IS LEFT, in `~/inkdrill-marks/table-rows.json`:

    88   the host line IS a table -- the crop shows a table. None of
         these is marked: all are suppressed by the "host region is not
         a line" clause, so no mark was ever drawn on one.
    236  the expression also occurs inside a table SOMEWHERE, but its
         host line is ordinary text. Not evidence of a fault; listed
         because it is the number a looser rule would report.
    30   the row's own LaTeX is a RULED or larger-than-4x4 array. The
         other 519 array rows are plain matrices up to 4x4, which are
         ordinary inline maths.

====================================================================
3. THE PLACEMENT EDGES DO NOT LEAN LEFT
====================================================================

HANDOVER has carried "the placement edges lean left (7 of 12 eye
errors)" as an open item since 658. Measured directly: for a marked
row, take the crop blobs whose centres fall inside the placed rectangle
and compare the rectangle's edges with the ink extent of exactly those
blobs.

    250 marked rows (150 of 0902.0431, 100 of gilmore), 400 dpi

    document      left edge - ink left      right edge - ink right
    0902.0431     median +0.0, mean -0.4    median +0.0, mean +0.2
    gilmore       median -1.0, mean -1.5    median +0.0, mean -0.2

    both edges left of the ink: 27 of 150 and 28 of 100 -- about what
    two independent signs would give.

The ink spans are 499 and 454 px at the median, so a systematic lean of
even a glyph would be visible. There is none. The 7-of-12 lean is a
property of the TWELVE KNOWN ERRORS, not of the placed population, and
the open item is re-worded accordingly.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEASURE. Whether a placement is on the RIGHT glyphs:
the rectangle is compared with the ink it covers, so a wholly misplaced
rectangle sitting neatly on the wrong expression still reads 0. It
refutes a systematic small lean, nothing else. The population is marked
rows only -- the ones the policy already trusts.
