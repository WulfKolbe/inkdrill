220 -- DO NOT DO AS WRITTEN. THREE REASONS, ANY ONE SUFFICIENT

The task gives the signature as "arrows and rules dominant, glyph
density low, no delimiter pair".

1 ONE CLAUSE IS SATISFIED BY 93% OF THE POPULATION. 203 measured
  "no delimiter pair" on 1,979 of 2,135 equation regions across five
  books. A clause that fires on 93% of everything carries almost no
  information, and a three-clause signature resting on it is really a
  two-clause one.

2 "RULES DOMINANT" CANNOT BE MEASURED WITH THE CURRENT DETECTOR.
  116 found `ink.rules[]` EMPTY on the Heim page -- 0 of 1,087
  regions pass `is_rule`, with 205 solid bars sitting at aspect 10-20
  against 3 at >= 20. 196 and 200 found 0 of 8 matrix rules, because
  a crossing fuses into one component that is neither bar-shaped nor
  thin-filled. So the channel this signature leans on reads zero on
  exactly the material it is meant to discriminate. 201 built the
  split that would fix half of it and it is not wired in.

3 THE PREMISE IS UNSETTLED, and this is the user's own point. Whether
  EQ0114 (TikZ), EQ1276 (division layout) and EQ0005/EQ0014
  (annotated tables) are REGION-TYPE errors -- MathPix labelled a
  figure as maths -- or READING errors -- it labelled the region
  correctly and read the content wrong -- decides what the detector
  is for. A region-type detector belongs upstream and its output is
  "this is not an equation"; a reading detector belongs here and its
  output is "this equation is wrong". They are not the same
  instrument and the four named cases have not been separated.

WHAT WOULD MAKE 220 DOABLE: settle (3) first, on the four named cases
alone, and re-take (2) with `crossing_rules` in the loop. Then the
signature can be written against a channel that can see its subject.

186 -- DO NOT DO AS WRITTEN, RE-STATE IT FIRST

186 asks to restate every gate using the floor of 7 against the
measured render-vs-scan distribution, POOLED rather than one
document.

The floor of 7 is sound and I would not reopen it: 6 -> 23 -> 7, and
the 23 came from a control group built on pdfdrill's SLT parser
collapsing `\begin{aligned}` to a single UNRESOLVED node, so 45% of
"content-identical" rows were nothing of the kind. 7 is the p95 over
the 813 rows carrying no multi-line or array environment, split
independently of their fix. That history is in `tools/findings.py`.

WHAT MAKES THE TASK'S FRAMING SUSPECT is 117, measured after 186 was
written. On the deficit-near-a-rule question the POOLED figure said
17.2% against an 11.5% null -- enrichment -- and the same data
compared PER ROW against each row's own density inverted it: below
its own null on 80 rows of 110, sign test z = -4.84. Sixteen rows
carrying 10% of the deficit and 60% of the near count produced the
pooled number.

"The pooled measurement, not one document" is exactly the instruction
that produced that inversion. Pooling is the right answer to "one
document dominates"; it is the wrong answer when the units differ in
the denominator that matters. 186 and 191 are the two halves of this
and 191 is the one that survives 117.

RECOMMENDATION: do 191 first. If its with-and-without split shows the
gates are stable, 186 reduces to a restatement and can be written
from 191's output. If it does not, 186 as written would restate every
gate on a number 117 has already shown can inverte.
