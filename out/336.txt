336 - what each crop contains that its base image does not
========================================================================

Eight confirmed pairs from pdfdrill's out/339.txt, the first pairs
this project has had where the base file and the crop of that same
figure are both known and no other base claims the crop.

SEVEN OF EIGHT DIFF CLEANLY. ONE DID NOT REGISTER, and that row is
the finding the task said would matter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
0. METHOD, AND THE ONE THING THAT CAN INVALIDATE IT

The crop is the printed figure PLUS whatever was drawn over it, so
the base's ink should be almost entirely covered by the crop's. The
base is placed inside the crop by a coarse scale/offset search
maximising that coverage; crop components the placed base does not
explain are the ADDITION.

COVERAGE IS REPORTED FOR EVERY PAIR. A bad registration produces a
large, confident, meaningless diff -- every component of a misplaced
figure looks added -- and in the output that is indistinguishable
from a heavily annotated figure. So a pair below 0.50 is declared
failed and its diff is NOT reported, rather than printed with a
caveat nobody reads.

2. THE DESCRIPTIONS

mpsapprox2 (cov 0.91) -- THE CLEANEST ANNOTATION SHAPE. Three
clusters ABOVE the figure, all centred (7%, 10%, 11% of crop width,
5-7% tall); six clusters BELOW, centred or slightly offset; three
small overlapping. Labels above and below a centred figure, which is
what a `\node` placed at north/south produces.

psi_N2 (cov 0.81) -- LEFT SIDE ONLY. Five of six clusters sit left of
the registered rectangle at offsets 0.45-0.58. One 10x8 cluster (7% x
6%) and the rest single components. An axis label or a row of marks
down one side.

rho_xi (cov 0.83) -- ONE CLUSTER, and the largest single addition
relative to its crop: 28x11 px, 19% of crop width, left at offset
0.80. A caption or label hard against the left edge.

passage (cov 0.61) -- SIXTEEN CLUSTERS, ALL OVERLAPPING OR RIGHT,
none centred. The largest are 34x7 (24% of width, 14 components) and
17x7 at offset 0.72. This is the lowest coverage of the seven that
registered, and the pattern -- many small overlapping clusters spread
across the figure -- is what an imperfect registration also produces.
Read it as the least reliable of the seven.

pt2 (cov 0.82) -- BELOW, CONSISTENTLY. Seven of eleven clusters below
the rectangle at offsets 0.31-0.39, all tiny (1x1 to 8x4). Two 20x17
overlapping clusters appear TWICE at identical extent and offset,
which is one object counted twice by the clusterer, not two objects.

vec_map (cov 0.85) -- THREE CLUSTERS, ALL OVERLAPPING, each 12% of
crop height, at offsets 0.16-0.30. Marks drawn onto the figure rather
than beside it.

entanglement (cov 0.86) -- FOURTEEN, mostly overlapping and small,
with three on the LEFT at offsets 0.58-0.66. The most annotated of
the eight by cluster count and the least by extent: nothing exceeds
9% of crop width.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE PAIR THAT FOUND NOTHING, AND WHY IT IS A FINDING

    ch1/bothways.jpg   base 725x538   crop 740x197
                       best placement scale 0.40, coverage 0.18

The base is 1.35:1 and the crop is 3.76:1. No scaling of the base
fits the crop's shape, so no placement covers more than a fifth of
its ink and the diff would have called almost the whole crop an
addition.

THE ASPECT RATIOS ARE THE EVIDENCE. A crop of an annotated figure is
the figure plus a margin -- its aspect can differ, but not by 2.8x.
Two readings fit:

  the crop shows only PART of this figure, so the base is not
  contained in it and no registration exists; or
  the pairing is wrong -- 339 matched this row by page and pixel
  size with one candidate crop and no competing claim, which is
  sound but is not a guarantee.

Either way this pair cannot be diffed by this method, and one of
eight is 12.5% of the evidence. It is reported rather than dropped
because a silent seven-of-eight would read as a method that works.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT THE SEVEN SHOW

Every one has additions, so the annotation IS separable when the
figure registers. The positions are not uniform:

    above and below, centred        1  (mpsapprox2)
    one side only                   2  (psi_N2, rho_xi)
    below only                      1  (pt2)
    overlapping the figure          3  (passage, vec_map, entanglement)

That is three of seven where the annotation is drawn ON the figure
rather than around it. A method that assumed annotations sit outside
the base rectangle would miss those three entirely, and 335's census
counted 224 inclusions with sibling nodes without distinguishing the
two placements.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

- That a cluster IS an annotation. It is crop ink the placed base
  does not explain. Registration error, JPEG artefacts at the figure
  edge and rescaling all leave residue, and passage's sixteen small
  overlapping clusters at coverage 0.61 are exactly what that residue
  looks like.
- That the counts are stable. Everything is measured at ~140 px wide
  after downsampling, so a cluster of one component is at the limit
  of what survives. The pt2 duplicate shows the clusterer emitting
  one object twice.
- Any rate. Eight pairs, one document, one author's figure style.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. POPULATION

The 8 matched pairs of pdfdrill out/339.json, document 2004.05631v1.
Bases from texsrc/figures, crops from texsrc/<uuid>/images. Both
sides binarised at threshold 200, downsampled to about 140 px wide
for the search. A component counts as added when under 35% of its ink
falls on the placed base dilated by one pixel.
