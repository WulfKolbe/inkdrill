247 - the diagram sweep, not built
========================================================================

246 DOES NOT SEPARATE THEM, so the sweep is not built and no count is
reported. This file exists to say that, and why, rather than to leave
the task looking unstarted.

THE MEASUREMENT: a TikZ rotation diagram against 75 display-equation
crops from three of the five books, on the six row-occupancy
statistics 246 asked for.

    statistic     diagram   percentile   equation range
    rows()          18.0       96.0         1 - 22
    comps/row med    2.0        6.7         1 - 45
    comps/row mean   5.7       21.3       2.2 - 45
    peak/median     13.5       98.7       1.0 - 14.9
    rows with 1      7.0       94.7         0 - 8
    y-spread       100.0        0.0       100 - 100

Every one overlaps. The equation population on its own reaches 22
rows, peak/median 14.9 and 8 single-component rows, so no value of
any single statistic puts the diagram outside it. The y-spread is an
identity -- `rows()` partitions its input, so the row extent equals
the ink extent for any input at all -- and carries no information.

A COMBINATION WOULD SEPARATE AND MUST NOT BE BUILT. Three statistics
put the diagram above the 94th percentile, so a conjunction of them
would isolate it. It would also be fitted to ONE positive example,
which is the objection that refused 220: a rule chosen because it
makes the known case come out right is not a measurement. 220 failed
by building before measuring; building here on a single instance
would be the same failure with a measurement attached to it.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THE SWEEP WORTH RUNNING: twenty hand-labelled
diagram regions and twenty hand-labelled equation regions, then these
six statistics over both. That labelling is exactly the unsettled
premise 220 was refused for -- whether the not-mathematics cases are
region-type errors or reading errors -- so the two tasks are blocked
on the same missing thing, and it is worth doing once for both.

See out/246.txt for the full measurement.
