590 - what a row manifest would replace, written BEFORE stage 2
====================================================================

Five inferences stand between `report.pdf` and a measured row. Three
are named in `inkmeasure`'s own docstring; two live on this side, in
`reportcompare` and the `compare` subcommand it drives. This says
which the manifest removes and which survive it, so stage 2 is
designed against the list rather than measured against it afterwards.

SUMMARY

  #  inference                        manifest?   why
  1  display-equations-only           REMOVES     the row says its kind
  2  legend rows kept                 REMOVES     the row says it exists
  3  table selected by ordinal        REMOVES     the row says its page
  4  the row lattice                  SURVIVES    a rect is not a boundary
  5  per-document column count        REMOVES     no table means no columns

  Four of five go. The one that stays is the one the residual is
  actually computed from, which is why "the legend becomes a colour
  choice" does not follow from four removals -- see the last section.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DISPLAY EQUATIONS ONLY                                  REMOVED

  Today: `inkconvert.identifiers` matches `\ident{...EQ<digits>}` and
  nothing else, so formulas, tables and image regions must not be
  measured, and the harness has to know which table on the page is the
  equations one before it can measure anything.

  With a manifest: each row carries `kind`, so the selection is a
  filter on a field. It also stops being a restriction -- the same
  measurement becomes available for TAB and FO rows, which is what
  498 needed and could not have (FO rows have no crop of their own).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE LEGEND ROWS STAY                                    REMOVED

  Today: `inkconvert.read_tsv` separates one all-zero row per page as
  the legend footer and counts display pages from them. 311c dropped
  them and left the consumer with no footers to find and a row count
  short by one per page. 238 then had to drop all-zero rows BEFORE the
  zip while keeping that one, and the vocabulary grew `absent` to stop
  a footer scoring as `clean`.

  With a manifest: the row list IS the row list. A page count comes
  from `page`, not from counting sentinels, and a row that is not in
  the manifest is not a row. The `absent` flag stays useful -- a cell
  with no ink on either side is still a real finding -- but it stops
  being load-bearing for pagination.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TABLE SELECTED BY ORDINAL                               REMOVED

  Today: a column count cannot pick a table when two share one (320),
  and contiguity cannot separate two ADJACENT tables of one width --
  0049's equations and formulas are both 5 columns, so inkdrill sees
  one 28-row run. `report.tables.json` says 1 then 27, and the split is
  taken on a page boundary because the builder puts a `\clearpage`
  between sections. That is three artefacts agreeing about a boundary
  none of them states.

  With a manifest: `page` plus `rect` places every row directly. No
  table has to be identified at all, so a document whose equations and
  formulas tables are the same width stops being a special case.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE ROW LATTICE                                        SURVIVES

  Today: `compare` runs `_table_cells` on the rendered page, clusters
  the ruling into a lattice, drops slivers under 40 px at 300 dpi, and
  refuses a page whose rows cover under half the table region. Each of
  those is a decision with a measured constant behind it, and each has
  been wrong once: 91 was a selection error (most holes, not largest
  box), the sliver floor lost a 1,597 px row on DTZ p022, and the
  coverage floor caught 0902.0431 p13 covering 1.5%.

  THE MANIFEST DOES NOT REPLACE THIS, and it is the important entry.
  A rect says where a row's CONTENT sits in PDF user space. It does not
  say where the cell boundary is, and the five-tuple is computed from
  the CELL -- the ruled box, including the whitespace the renderer put
  round the content. Two different rectangles:

      manifest rect     the ink's own extent, from the builder
      lattice cell      the ruled box, from the raster

  Measuring the manifest rect instead would change every number in the
  corpus, because a tighter crop has different components, different
  centring and different offsets. So the lattice stays, and the
  manifest's value here is as a CHECK on it: a lattice row whose box
  does not contain its manifest rect is a detection error, reported
  rather than silently measured. That is a new guard the manifest
  makes possible, not an inference it removes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE PER-DOCUMENT COLUMN COUNT                           REMOVED

  Today: `target_columns` reads the count from the document's own
  report.tex, because a constant was wrong twice in one day -- 5 before
  pdfdrill's 099 added a Confidence column, 6 after, and the corpus
  holds both eras. 496 measured 843 documents at 6 and 492 with no scan
  column at all. `probe` then renders every page at 150 dpi to find the
  pages whose lattice has that many columns, which is most of the wall
  clock of a run and needed its own cache.

  THE SIX-COLUMN ASSUMPTION GOES WITH IT. It is not a separate
  inference; it is what the column count is FOR -- picking the display
  pages. If a row states its page, there is nothing to probe: the page
  list is `{row.page for row in manifest}`, the probe disappears, and
  with it the cache, the era problem, and 498's finding that a
  `Rendered`-without-`Scan image` header must be skipped over.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR 583's CONCLUSION

583 proposes that if the residuals agree both ways, the legend and the
two-phase build become a colour choice. Four of the five inferences do
go, and #2 in particular means the legend stops carrying pagination.

But #4 says the residual itself is still computed from the lattice
cell, not from the manifest rect. So "the residuals agree" would be
testing that the manifest finds the SAME ROWS, not that it supplies
the same GEOMETRY -- and those are different claims. The stronger one
is available and worth asking for in stage 2: if the manifest carried
the CELL rect as well as the content rect, #4 would move from SURVIVES
to REMOVED and the lattice could be dropped entirely.

That is the one design note this list produces, and it is cheap to act
on before stage 2 rather than after: the builder knows the cell box, it
is a longtable cell it just typeset, and recovering it from the raster
afterwards is the thing this whole inventory is trying to stop doing.
