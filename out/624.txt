2501.06662, report.pdf page 2, 300 dpi, A3 landscape 1190.55 x 841.89

them, and the five-tuple is not at fault -- because THE SCAN COLUMN IS
EMPTY. The images are on page 2, they are placed at the correct
VERTICAL position for their rows, and they are drawn in the wrong
COLUMN: at the left edge of the table, across columns 0 to 3.

2. WHY IT IS NOT ANY OF THE THREE

  NOT the five-tuple. The count above is a direct byte count over the
  rect. It is zero. Nothing downstream of the crop can be blamed for a
  zero that is already zero in the raster.

  NOT the rects. Column 5's data rects share their y-bands EXACTLY
  with column 4, which reads 6281, 4984 and 5822 over the same rows.
  They are 1537 px wide, on-page (the raster is 4961 wide), non-empty,
  and in ascending order. A shifted or zero-height rect cannot produce
  a neighbour that reads correctly on the same band.

  NOT ink outside the rects. In row 1's band the only ink anywhere to
  the right of x=3199 is at x 4736..4737 -- the table's right-hand
  rule, two pixels of it. And it is not a threshold artefact: sampled
  over the whole col-5 data band the grey histogram is 85,158 pixels
  of value 255 and NOTHING else. Darkest value 255.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT IT IS: THE IMAGES ARE DRAWN IN THE WRONG COLUMN

`pdfimages -list` says page 2 carries three image objects, and
`mutool trace` says five image placements. All five are at

    x = 59.825 bp, extending to at most 405.65 bp

against the lattice's own columns, which agree with the PDF's vertical
rules to a fraction of a point:

    col 0    54.00 .. 122.40 bp        rules: 53.65
    col 1   123.12 .. 154.56           122.69
    col 2   155.28 .. 203.76           154.89
    col 3   204.48 .. 476.88           204.09
    col 4   477.60 .. 767.04           477.24
    col 5   767.76 .. 1136.64          767.38, 1136.90

So every image begins inside COLUMN 0 and spills across columns 1, 2
and into 3. None of them is within 360 bp of the Scan column.

AND THE VERTICAL POSITION IS CORRECT. Each image falls inside the row
band it belongs to:

    row 1   band  63.84 ..  183.12 bp     image 132.1 .. 179.5
    row 2   band 183.60 ..  256.08        image 219.8 .. 252.5
    row 3   band 256.56 ..  367.44        image 307.4 .. 364.0
    row 4   band 368.16 ..  651.36        image 517.3 .. 647.8
    row 5   band 651.84 ..  785.28        image 735.1 .. 781.8

Right row, wrong column, five times out of five. The crops exist on
disk (445 files; the five identifiers on this page all resolve, 17 KB
to 53 KB each) and report.tex references them by name, so this is not
a missing file and not a failed \includegraphics -- the image is drawn,
at the wrong x.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHERE THE FIX GOES

Not in the lattice and not in the five-tuple: both are reporting the
raster correctly. It is in what the emitter typesets.

The suspect is visible in report.tex. The last cell of each row reads

    & \pdcolpos{col00006}\rule{0pt}{0.6mm}\\[-0.6mm]\includegraphics
      [width=120.0mm]{report-crops/2501.06662_EQ0051.jpg}...

with a `\\[-0.6mm]` between the strut and the image, in a
`p{126mm}` column. Naming it as the cause is pdfdrill's to confirm --
what is measured here is that the image lands at the table's left
edge at the row's correct height, which is the signature of content
leaving its cell horizontally while keeping its place vertically.

TWO THINGS SEEN IN PASSING, both worth a look on that side:

  report.log carries "LaTeX Warning: Label `rul00001' multiply
  defined", repeatedly. That is 608 #3's problem -- a label in a
  repeating head -- present in this build.

  Page 3's lattice reports 6 rows x FIVE columns where page 2 reports
  six, in the same report. Its last column also reads header-only.
  A column count that changes between pages of one table is the
  instability 601 section 4 recorded; here it may be downstream of
  the same misplacement.
